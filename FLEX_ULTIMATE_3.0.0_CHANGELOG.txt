================================================================================
FLEX ULTIMATE FOR PREMIERE PRO - WHAT'S NEW
Version 3.0.0  -  November 2026
================================================================================

The Assets tab gets 1,596 new templates that play on hover before you download,
search across every tab and Import MOGRT. SS To Premiere comes over from GUI Pro,
and Flex Studio reaches 1.0.0.

BEFORE YOU START
- Install over your current version with your usual ZXP installer, then restart
  Premiere Pro once (the timeline fixes load when Premiere starts).
- Your settings, presets, library and saved styles are kept, with one exception:
  Retakes moves to Gemini 3.5 Transcribe once (see below). You can pick another
  engine again.
- Flex Studio is not code-signed on Windows yet, so Windows may show a SmartScreen
  warning.
- New interface text stays in English until the next translation pass.

NEW: ASSETS TAB
- 1,596 new templates. Added to existing tabs: Text Presets 194, Text Animation
  166, Hook & Captions 20, Transitions 5.
- 11 new tabs: Infographics 940 (34 groups), Instagram Stories 77, Social Media
  Elements 53, Match Cuts 31, Lower Thirds 27, Titles & Documentary 25, YouTube
  Subscribe 20, Instagram Posts & Promo 10, Slideshows 10, Counters 9, Messages &
  Notifications 9.
- Each new template shows its picture and plays on hover before it is on your
  computer. Autoplay runs up to 16 tiles on screen, and stops while your pointer
  is outside the panel so Premiere stays free.
- Download only the group you have open: Flags alone is 256 templates and 1.4 GB,
  instead of the whole tab. The toast shows each template's file size.
- Search every template in every tab by name, group, tab or file name. A whole
  tab or group by that name is a one-click chip.
- Import MOGRT: add your own files or a whole folder, or drop them on the grid.
  Flex keeps a copy under My Templates and renders a preview. Rendering needs
  After Effects and ffmpeg on this computer. A template made in Premiere keeps its
  picture and says why.
- Right-click a downloaded template: Render preview, Show the file.
- A SLOTS mark shows which templates take your own images or video. Select the
  clip on the timeline and drag your media onto them in Properties or Essential
  Graphics. Example: in Slideshows, Carousel Background is only a colour, and the
  Carousel Scenes take 12 to 36 images or videos.
- Tabs stay loaded, so going back to one is quick and keeps your scroll position.
- The tabs sit in a sidebar or along the top: two icons beside AUTOPLAY switch
  them, and your choice is remembered.
- Tile names sit under the picture instead of covering it, and Light Leak no
  longer crops its videos.

NEW: SS TO PREMIERE (FX & ADV RIGS)
- GUI Pro's SS To AE comes to Premiere. A screenshot becomes an animated sequence.
- It builds a new bin and a new sequence at the screenshot's size, with each
  element on its own track, animated with Motion and Opacity keys. Choose a limit
  of 30, 60 or 90 tracks; small neighbouring elements share a track.
- Import AI / PSD and Reset Animation stay in the After Effects version.

CHANGED
- Organize Project: new option, on by default, Leave my own bins and imported
  folders alone. Turning it off warns you first. The two older ORGANIZE PROJECT
  buttons now open the FX & ADV RIGS page.
- Retakes moves to Gemini 3.5 Transcribe once. It uploads the clip's audio, uses
  Flex AI credits and needs internet. Pick another engine and your choice sticks.
  If the cloud run fails, it says why and offers FAST AI · LARGE V3 TURBO on your
  computer.
- FX & ADV RIGS: Flex Studio and Motion Tracking are now two rows. A track sent
  back from Flex Studio lands on Motion Tracking.
- Curve editor: click any parameter number and type it. DEF, BASE or MINE stays
  selected after a restart.
- Six new Awakened Eyes themes: Eclipse, Clockwork, Prism, Viper, Moth and Abyss.

NEW: FLEX STUDIO 1.0.0
- Out of beta. Your sessions, models and macOS permissions carry over, and the
  update removes the beta. Installing it in the same folder replaces the older
  standalone Flex STUDIO grading app.
- Studio Link: Open in Flex Studio puts the Flex STUDIO effect on the clip and
  links it. Every Save Grade then shows there, with no Apply press. It needs the
  Flex STUDIO effect 1.1.0: FX & ADV RIGS > Flex Studio installs or updates it, then
  restart Premiere Pro. A copy in the old folder is moved, which asks for your
  password once. An effect you graded by hand is left alone.
- Object Brush: SAM 2.1 Fast is the default on computers with a GPU path (about 8
  FPS against 0.9 before, measured on an Apple M5 with 24 GB). Hover lights up the
  object under the pointer. MODEL menu: SAM 2.1 Fast, SAM 2.1 and SAM 3 (about a 1
  GB download, needs 16 GB of RAM, recommended from 24 GB with a GPU).
- COLOR GRADING > ADJUSTMENT, laid out like Lumetri Color: Basic Correction,
  Creative, Curves, Color Wheels, HSL Secondary and Vignette.
- In Premiere the Flex STUDIO effect stays on the CPU, with a faster path on Mac.
- Frames read faster on Mac, about 1.75x for 4K HEVC.
- Your panel theme comes along when a session is opened from the panel.

FIXED
- Smart Synchronise now syncs recordings made on two devices apart in a room, such
  as an iPhone video and an iPad voice memo.
- The location privacy card no longer asks again on later launches.
- The old licence box no longer shows over the panel on a slow launch.
- Flex Studio: a pen shape no longer disappears when you press V or pick another
  tool. With 3 or more points it becomes a new layer.

UNCHANGED
- Your licence, credits and saved work. Everything not listed here works as it
  did in 2.3.0.
