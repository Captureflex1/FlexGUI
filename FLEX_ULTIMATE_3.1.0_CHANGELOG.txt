================================================================================
FLEX ULTIMATE FOR PREMIERE PRO - WHAT'S NEW
Version 3.1.0  -  November 2026
================================================================================

AutoCaptions gets 21 new animated looks that come in as real text clips you
can edit word by word. Auto Clipper turns a long video into Shorts with
captions in one go. The Graph Editor now shapes Premiere's own keyframe
handles instead of adding keyframes in between. Plus 83 new templates in
Assets and a Local mode repair button.

BEFORE YOU START
- Windows: quit Premiere Pro completely before you install.
- Install over your current version with your usual ZXP installer, then restart
  Premiere Pro once.
- Your settings, theme, presets and downloaded templates are kept.

NEW: 21 ANIMATED CAPTION LOOKS AS TEXT CLIPS (AUTOCAPTIONS > LOOK)
- New looks in the Real Estate group: Showcase, Open House, Skyline, Spark
  Count, Headline, Gilded, Chrome Line, Remix, Velvet, Springboard,
  Highlighter, Staircase, Boardroom, Marquee, Widescreen, Cockpit, Slide Deck,
  Neon Stack, Scrapbook, Stage Light and Orbit.
- Output: Text clips (new) puts every word on its own Premiere text clip,
  stacked on new tracks above your edit (your clips are never moved or
  covered). Each word keeps its moves as keyframes and stays editable in
  Essential Graphics. Glows, sparks and shapes sit on one clip under the
  words. Remove takes them all out again.
- Output: Video clip makes one drawn clip, as before. Showcase, Open House
  and Skyline put your own footage inside the big words in this mode.
- A look whose fonts you do not have installs them for you (all free fonts).
  Restart Premiere Pro once after that.

NEW: AUTO CLIPPER (FX & ADV RIGS > AUTO CLIPPER)
- Long video in, Shorts out: select the video, press CREATE SHORTS. It
  transcribes the video, picks the best moments, makes one 1080x1920
  sequence per moment in a "Flex Auto Clipper" bin and captions each one with
  a look from Assets > Hook & Captions (editable in Essential Graphics).
- Cloud (Flex AI picks the moments, uses credits) or Local (on your computer,
  no internet). Optional MP4 export through Media Encoder.
- Each Short gets a ready title, description and hashtags to copy.
- Your own sequences are never changed.

NEW: GRAPH EDITOR KEYS = HANDLES / SMART / BAKED
- HANDLES (the new default): your curve is put on Premiere's own keyframe
  handles. No keyframes are added in between.
- SMART: adds only the few keyframes a strong curve needs (elastic, bounce).
- BAKED: the old way, a keyframe on every frame.
- Works on Position and Anchor Point too.
- The Graph Editor now opens on the BASE library with PARAMETERS closed, and
  remembers where you leave it.

NEW: 83 TEMPLATES IN ASSETS
- Slideshows: Colorful, Dynamic and Modern Liquid Slideshows (10 -> 40).
- Transitions: Cartoon, Liquid Motion, Neon, Seamless and Splash Liquid
  Transitions (72 -> 125).

NEW: LOCAL MODE "FIX IT" (AUTOCAPTIONS)
- When Local mode will not run, FIX IT sets up its own private Python,
  Whisper and ffmpeg for Flex, with a repair for each failure it finds.
  Nothing else on your computer is changed.

CHANGED
- Tooltips are off by default. Turn them back on in Settings > Tooltips.
  When they are off, no tip shows at all (before, a plain hover tip still
  came up after a long hover).
- The AutoCaptions tab icon is now "Aa".

FIXED
- Downloader: "Download a section" downloaded the whole video when the Flex
  Downloader app was installed. It now downloads the section.

UNCHANGED
- Everything else: AutoCaptions, Smart Sync, Motion Tracking, Audio Studio,
  themes and your saved settings.
