================================================================================
FLEX GUI PRO FOR AFTER EFFECTS - WHAT'S NEW
Version 9.5.3  -  November 2026
================================================================================

Morph Maniac morphs one shape, text or logo into another inside the Shape tab,
and the AI tab's agents can listen, read a waveform and wait for your OK.

BEFORE YOU START
- Install over your current version with your usual ZXP installer, then restart
  After Effects once.
- Your settings, theme and presets are kept.
- New interface text stays in English until the next translation pass.

NEW: MORPH MANIAC (SHAPE > MORPH MANIAC)
- Morph a shape layer, text, an Illustrator, SVG or PDF layer, or masks into
  another one. The result is a normal shape layer with path keyframes.
- Both ends stay exact: the first frame is your first shape, the last frame is
  your second.
- Pins: mark which point should travel to which, when the automatic match is
  not the one you want.
- Sequence: morph through several shapes one after another.
- Bounce: an overshoot at the end of each morph, with its own sliders.
- Shapes with holes keep their holes. Fills are solid colours for now.

NEW IN THE AI TAB
- Flex Agents 1.2: an AI app working in After Effects can now transcribe on
  this computer (Whisper, no credits) and look at frames, audio and a waveform.
  Long jobs report back instead of timing out.
- Review changes (on by default with Flex Agents): each change an agent wants
  to make waits for you with a Before / After picture: Keep, Undo or Keep all.
- The chat shows what an agent did as labelled rows: frames, audio, transcribe,
  waveform.

CHANGED
- FlexBrowser: when its docked panel is not installed yet, the FlexBrowser tab
  now downloads and installs it for you (your password once, the first time a
  Flex plug-in goes into After Effects), then offers to restart After Effects.
  No separate installer to look for.

UNCHANGED
- Flex Studio: Transform, Corner Pin, Stabilize, Mask, Warp and Import render
  work as before.
- Everything else, and your saved settings.
