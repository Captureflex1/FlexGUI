FLEX ULTIMATE FOR PREMIERE PRO - WHAT'S NEW
Version 2.2.4  -  October 2026

The MOTION TRACKER has a new engine that follows faces, cartoons and handheld
shots far more reliably. CAPTION STACK gets stroke, drop shadow,
colours and fonts, drawn into the captions so they look the same in Premiere.
AUTO CAPTIONS opens five more speech engines and gets a RESET CACHE button, the
text anchor point snaps to the visible text, and every FX & ADV RIGS page has a
cleaner look.

BEFORE YOU START
- Install over your current version with your usual ZXP installer, then restart
  Premiere Pro once.
- Your quick effects, presets, settings and downloaded templates are kept.

NEW: MOTION TRACKER ENGINE (FX & ADV RIGS)
- A new tracking engine. It follows animated characters, faces, handheld shots,
  fast pans, zooms and rolls far more reliably. On a 2D-animated
  dance where the old tracker lost the face after 9 frames, the new one follows
  it to the last frame.
- When the target is covered or leaves the frame, the tracker follows the shot
  around it, marks those frames UNCERTAIN, and looks for the target again when it
  comes back.
- CORNER PIN: place the four corners in any order, including Premiere's own
  Corner Pin order. A pin that slides out of the frame no longer sends its
  corners flying.
- Faster: Accurate now takes well under half the time it used to.
- Paste, Stabilize and Smart keyframes work as before.

NEW: CAPTION STACK STYLE
- A STYLE card under Look: FONT (the look's own, Bold, Tall, Script, or any font
  installed on your computer), LETTER CASE, TEXT and ACCENT colour, the look's
  GLOW on or off, STROKE (width and colour) and DROP SHADOW (distance, blur,
  opacity, angle and colour).
- The style is drawn into the captions themselves. Caption Stack places captions
  as images, so Premiere's text styling cannot change them; set it here and the
  preview, the look thumbnails and the timeline all match.

NEW: FIVE MORE SPEECH ENGINES IN AUTO CAPTIONS
- Qwen3-ASR, Moonshine, NVIDIA Canary, AI4Bharat IndicConformer and SraVaani are
  now in the model menu (they were COMING SOON). The first time you use one, it
  sets itself up (a few minutes, plus the model download).
- Moonshine is the fastest for English. For Hindi, IndicConformer and Qwen3-ASR
  gave the best results in our tests.
- IndicConformer and SraVaani are gated by their makers on Hugging Face: they need
  a free Hugging Face account and access token the first time. The panel shows
  the steps if the token is missing.
- Commas and full stops stay attached to their word (some engines showed them as
  words of their own), and Moonshine no longer repeats words.

NEW: RESET CACHE (AUTO CAPTIONS)
- Click the LOCAL chip at the top of Auto Captions, then RESET CACHE at the
  bottom of Engine & models. It deletes Auto Captions' temporary files and its
  saved Python check, then checks Python again. It asks first and lists what goes.
- Your downloaded models and your settings are kept.

CHANGED: TEXT ANCHOR POINT
- The anchor grid on text and graphic clips now snaps to the text you can see
  (its corners, edges and centre) instead of the corners of the whole frame.
  Other clips work as before.

CHANGED: FX & ADV RIGS LOOK
- Motion Tracker, Caption Stack, Sequence Resizer and Organize Project have a
  cleaner look: bigger labels, clearer choices, switches and sliders.

FIXED
- QWEN3-ASR could stop with "check_model_inputs() missing 1 required positional
  argument" after its Python packages were updated. It now repairs itself the
  next time you use it.

UNCHANGED
- Licensing, activation and every link work exactly as before.
- Every other tab and tool works exactly as in 2.2.3.
