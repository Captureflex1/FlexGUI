FLEX ULTIMATE FOR PREMIERE PRO - WHAT'S NEW
Version 2.2.3  -  October 2026

SMART SYNCHRONISE is rebuilt and now really syncs: pieces of the same recording
snap back to their exact frame, and different cameras and recorders are matched
by their audio. AUTO CUT is faster and shows its progress step by step, REMOVE
RETAKES transcribes first with your Auto Captions model, CAPTION STACK takes your
own text, and AUTO CAPTIONS shows which model is working and how far it is.
Plus template previews in Assets and a long list of fixes.

BEFORE YOU START
- Install over your current version with your usual ZXP installer, then restart
  Premiere Pro once.
- Your quick effects, presets, settings and downloaded templates are kept.

NEW: AUTO CUT SHOWS ITS PROGRESS (SILENCE & CUT)
- A progress bar under AUTO CUT shows each step: backing up the sequence,
  TRIMMING the gaps, a countdown "STARTING RIPPLE DELETE IN X S", then RIPPLE
  DELETING and DONE. On a long timeline Premiere needs a moment before the cuts
  can be removed; you now see that wait counting down instead of a frozen panel.
- REMOVE RETAKES shows the same bar.

NEW: REMOVE RETAKES TRANSCRIBES FIRST
- Retakes now opens on TRANSCRIBE: the clip is transcribed with the model you
  picked in Auto Captions, with a live progress line (downloading the model,
  transcribing 45%, finishing), and the repeated takes are found in that
  transcript.
- HINDI AND HINGLISH: restart words like "phir se" and "dobara" are now
  recognised.

NEW: CAPTION STACK - CUSTOM TEXT
- Type or paste your own text. If the clip has been transcribed, your words take
  the timing of what was said; otherwise they are spread over the clip.
- TRANSCRIBE SELECTED CLIP now uses the Auto Captions engine, shows its
  progress, and tells you clearly when something goes wrong.

NEW: AUTO CAPTIONS SHOWS WHAT IT IS DOING
- The progress card names the model you picked ("Loading Whisper Large V3
  Turbo") and shows what is really happening: downloading the model with size
  and speed on first use, then "Transcribing... 50%" with how much audio is done.
  It no longer sits at 30%.
- CUSTOM WORDS: add names and words Whisper keeps getting wrong, and they are
  spelled your way.
- The model menu is ranked by accuracy. Newer speech engines are listed as
  COMING SOON.

NEW: TEMPLATE PREVIEWS IN ASSETS
- Hover a template or sound to preview it: audio plays with its waveform, and
  templates that include a preview video play it.
- AUTOPLAY plays the previews in view by themselves; switch it off in the
  toolbar.

CHANGED: SMART SYNCHRONISE (GENERAL TAB)
- PIECES OF THE SAME RECORDING are put back exactly by their source time, frame
  accurate, with no audio guessing. Copies of the same moment go on their own
  tracks, and pieces cut from each other continue on the same track.
- DIFFERENT CAMERAS AND RECORDERS are matched by their sound, each against
  everything already placed, and every match is double-checked on the waveform.
  A recording it cannot match safely is reported, never placed at a wrong spot.
- STEREO STAYS STEREO: one clip on one track, no more dozens of mono tracks.
- ANY FRAME RATE: phone footage at 59.99 fps no longer stops it with "This
  sequence has an unusual frame rate".
- It says what it did: how many pieces, how many recordings, what was matched
  by audio and what was skipped and why. Your original sequence is unchanged.

CHANGED: AUTO CUT IS FASTER
- Far fewer round trips to Premiere per cut, so long clips finish sooner. Same
  cuts as before.

CHANGED: AUTOCAMERA
- Fixed several cases where camera switching did not apply, and two mics
  recorded into one file are now handled when only one person talks in the
  range you picked.

CHANGED: GRAPH EDITOR
- The curve library now sits beside the graph by default.

CHANGED: SMOOTHER PANEL
- Less work in the background while you edit, so Premiere Pro stays more
  responsive.

FIXED
- FREEZE FRAME took the first frame of the timeline instead of the frame at the
  playhead.
- QUICK EFFECTS on a duplicated clip went onto the original clip below it.
- IMPORTING FROM ASSETS could place the template on top of clips already on the
  timeline. It now goes where there is room.
- THE LIBRARY FOLDER went back to the default location after a restart.
- GRAPH EDITOR could stay on "Reading the selected clip..." and then answer
  APPLY with "Select a clip with keyframes in the timeline first".
- WINDOWS: file and folder pickers use the standard modern Windows dialog.

UNCHANGED
- Licensing, activation and every link work exactly as before.
- Every other tab and tool works exactly as in 2.2.2.
