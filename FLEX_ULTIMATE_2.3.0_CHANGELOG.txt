================================================================================
FLEX ULTIMATE FOR PREMIERE PRO - WHAT'S NEW
Version 2.3.0  -  October 2026
================================================================================

Caption Stack moves into AutoCaptions with per-word styles, line layouts,
word motion and caption sounds. UN-NEST and blend modes work on Premiere 26.5,
AUTO CUT is one press, Audio Studio is tidier, and Flex Studio (Beta) opens
your clip from the panel.

BEFORE YOU START
- Install over your current version with your usual ZXP installer, then restart
  Premiere Pro once (the timeline fixes load when Premiere starts).
- Your settings, presets, library and saved styles are kept, with two
  exceptions: AutoCaptions switches to Gemini 3.5 Transcribe once, and Retakes
  switches to FAST · LARGE V3 TURBO once (see below). You can pick your old
  engine again in both.

NEW: ANIMATED CAPTIONS INSIDE AUTOCAPTIONS
- The AutoCaptions tab now reads Captions | Animated | Editor, with Word Pop and
  Transcript under More. Animated is the Caption Stack, right in the tab
  (FX & ADV RIGS > Caption Stack opens it there too).
- Word style: click a word in the preview and give it its own fill, stroke,
  box and shadow colours, its own font and a finish (Flat, Shine or Glow).
  Copy and paste a style, give it to the whole caption, or reset it. With All
  captions picked it styles the word in that place in every caption (every
  hero word, every first small word, and so on), not the same word wherever
  you say it. Pick This caption to change only the caption on screen; those
  styles are cleared when you load new words or change Words per group. Hero
  word still makes any word the big word of its caption.
- Line layouts: Stack, Over (small words above the hero word), Under, Beside
  and Line (every word on one line), for one caption or all of them.
- Motion per word: Fade, Pop, Bounce, Rise, Drop, From left, From right, Type,
  Flash, Shake or Blur in, with a strength setting. Press the play button in
  Animated's Editor card to preview it.
- Word styles and word motion reach the timeline with the Images output (the
  Output card starts on Editable MOGRT on Premiere 2026; one click switches
  to Images).
- Caption sounds: Pop, Tick or Swoosh on each caption or each word, placed on
  a free audio track.

NEW: AUTOCAPTIONS - GEMINI 3.5 TRANSCRIBE
- A cloud engine, now the recommended one, selected once for everyone. It
  uploads your audio to Flex AI, uses Flex AI credits and needs internet.
- To caption offline for free, pick a Whisper engine again; your choice is
  then kept. Translate To needs Whisper: with Gemini 3.5 Transcribe the
  captions stay in the language you spoke.
- When the caption server is busy, the progress card shows how long to wait
  and your place in line, and counts down. Your captions start by themselves;
  Cancel takes you out of the line.

NEW: AUTOCAPTIONS - YOUR SCRIPT
- A "Your script" field under Language. Paste the script you read (or load a
  text file) and the captions take its spelling and wording wherever you said
  those words, so names and brands come out the way you wrote them.
- What you really said usually wins: a clearly different word stays as you
  said it, ad-libs are kept, and common opposites (before/after, on/off) and
  different numbers are not swapped in. Words that look alike, and numbers you
  say as words, can still take the script's version, so check those captions.
- If the script does not match the recording, it is not used and the panel
  tells you so. It is not applied to translated captions.
- It works with Generate in Captions. For animated captions, generate in
  Captions first, then press Last Auto Captions in Animated (Animated's own
  Transcribe does not use the script).

CHANGED: AUTOCAPTIONS
- Nothing selected, and no clip in the Source card yet? The panel asks whether
  to caption the whole timeline. It mixes every unmuted audio track and
  captions from the start of the sequence. Audio inside nested sequences is
  not included: un-nest or render those first. Once the Source card shows a
  clip (after Detect, Browse or a run), Generate captions that clip again;
  close and reopen the panel to get the whole-timeline question back.
- Finished captions are also saved next to your project, in a "Flex Captions"
  folder (in Documents > Flex Captions while the project is not saved yet),
  and IMPORT CAPTIONS uses that copy, so captions no longer go offline when
  you reopen Premiere.
- Captioning the same audio again with the same settings reuses the transcript:
  no waiting, no upload, no credits used. Tick "Transcribe again" (under More
  options) for a fresh one.
- A simpler tab: the less used settings sit under More options.

CHANGED: AUTO CUT, RETAKES AND THE TIMELINE CARDS
- AUTO CUT is one press: select a clip and, if it has not been analysed yet,
  AUTO CUT analyses it itself, then cuts. For a whole sequence, press ANALYZE
  SEQUENCE first, then AUTO CUT. Dragging a FINE TUNE value now moves the gaps
  too (before, the number changed but AUTO CUT still cut the old gaps).
- Retakes now finds a line you cut short and then started again, and no longer
  adds "Thank you." or "Thanks for watching" over room tone.
- Retakes now opens on FAST · LARGE V3 TURBO (a one-time switch). It also
  moves Retakes off TRANSCRIBE or FLEX AI · CLOUD, even if you picked one
  yourself; pick it again if you want it back. MEDIUM or LARGE V3 picked for
  Retakes is kept. The first scan downloads the model (about 1.6 GB) if it is
  not on your computer yet.
- The General TRANSFORM card and UN-NEST button, Audio Studio's clip line and
  the Source card in Mark Audio Beats follow your timeline selection by
  themselves (no Refresh needed).

CHANGED: AUDIO STUDIO
- Six sub-tabs instead of one long page: PRESETS, EFFECTS, CLEAN UP, LEVELS,
  TIME & PITCH and STEMS. Every effect has one place to add it, and one bar at
  the bottom holds the clip, Preview and the main button.
- STEMS shows a "Last split" list: each stem and the track it was placed on.

CHANGED: LIBRARY AND ASSETS
- The Library now lives in Nest Saver (NEST DECK | LIBRARY) with a cleaner
  layout: search, sort, + IMPORT, categories and a selection bar only while
  something is selected. The Assets tab keeps Presets.

NEW: FLEX STUDIO (BETA) FROM PREMIERE
- FX & ADV RIGS > Flex Studio: Open in Flex Studio works on a video clip (not a
  nest, title, still or reversed clip). It launches the app at once while the
  panel reads its own frames in the background. In the app, APPLY TO PREMIERE
  sends the track back to the Track card, ready for Paste tracking or
  Stabilize.
- When the app is not on your computer, the button reads DOWNLOAD FLEX STUDIO
  (BETA) with its size. One click downloads it, then asks "Install it now?"
  (Install / Not now). It goes into Applications (Mac) or your Programs folder
  (Windows): no password needed.
- If an older Flex Studio is already installed (for example from Flex GUI Pro),
  an UPDATE button appears beside Open: older versions cannot send a track
  back to Premiere.
- The app (2.0.0-beta.2) has PRO, TRACK, ROTOSCOPE and COLOR GRADING workspaces,
  and clips open at once.

FIXED
- UN-NEST refused every nest on Premiere 26.5 ("both the nest and the clip use
  a blend mode"), refused nests with sound, and could report "Premiere did not
  move" a clip it had moved. All three work again on 26.5, and NEST SEP too.
- Blend modes on Premiere 26.5: a light leak set to Screen turned back to
  Normal as soon as you selected the clip. Blend modes now stay, and UN-NEST
  carries them over (Premiere Pro in English; with Premiere in another
  language a blend mode Flex sets can still go back to Normal for now).
- The AUTOSAVE chip sat on a red "0:00" in Premiere. It now shows when the
  project was last saved and turns red only after a failed save; Save and Flex
  Auto-Save save the Premiere project directly.
- The Caption Stack editor showed a black preview with no reason. It now says
  why there is no picture (no transcript yet, or no video under the captions).
- Theme effects (trails, button effects) were missing on many Windows PCs.
  With Windows animation effects off, the anime themes now run a lighter
  version (a short swing on each click, no cursor trail) instead of nothing,
  and a graphics card that reads slow while the panel is starting no longer
  gets its effects switched off as if it had no GPU.
- The language search said "Search 133 languages"; it is 132.

NEW: OPTIONAL LOCATION FOR LICENCE PROTECTION
- The panel asks once whether you allow it to send this computer's location
  (or, when your system can't give one, roughly your city from your internet
  connection), to protect your licence: to spot one key used from far-apart
  places at the same time. It is optional: "No thanks" keeps every feature
  working.
- Change your mind any time: Flex Settings > PRIVACY. Turning it off also
  deletes what was stored for your licence and computer.
- Kept for 12 months, then deleted. Never sold, and never shared with anyone
  else: our hosting provider only stores it for us, and we would hand it over
  only if the law requires it. Details: captureflex.in/privacy

UNCHANGED
- Licensing, activation and every link work exactly as before.
- Every other tab and tool works exactly as in 2.2.9.
