================================================================================
FLEX GUI PRO FOR AFTER EFFECTS - WHAT'S NEW
Version 9.2.6  -  October 2026
================================================================================

FLEX STUDIO (BETA) brings planar tracking, roto and stabilizing to After
Effects: track in the new Flex Studio app, then attach the track to your
layers from FX & ADV RIGS. Force Preview's engine now installs from inside the
panel, AUTOCAPTIONS gets five more speech engines and a progress card that
shows what it is really doing, and now gives every speech model only your
audio and the language. The new UI SCALE setting makes the whole panel
smaller or bigger. Plus a lighter panel and several fixes.

BEFORE YOU START
- Install over your current version with your usual ZXP installer, then restart
  After Effects once.
- Your theme, presets and saved expressions are kept. Three folder choices
  may need picking once more (see FIXED).
- Flex Studio (Beta) and the Flex Preview engine are not inside this ZXP. The
  panel downloads them when you press INSTALL, so you need to be online:
    Flex Studio (Beta) app: about 110-120 MB on a Mac, about 140 MB on Windows
      (the INSTALL button shows the exact size)
    Object Brush model in Flex Studio: about 190 MB, the first time you use it
    Flex Preview engine for Force Preview Render: under 1 MB
- The first time Flex puts a plug-in into After Effects (the Flex Studio Warp
  effect or the Flex Preview engine), your computer asks for permission once:
  your Mac password, or the Windows administrator prompt. Later installs and
  updates do not ask again. The Flex Studio app itself needs no password.
- After Effects only loads new plug-ins when it starts. After a plug-in
  install, press RESTART AFTER EFFECTS (After Effects asks you to save first),
  or LATER and restart it yourself.
- WINDOWS: the in-panel installs and the Windows version of Flex Studio (Beta)
  are brand new and have not been tested on a Windows PC yet. The Flex Studio
  app is not code-signed yet, so Windows may show a warning when it first
  opens. If an install fails, tell us on our Discord; the Flex Preview zip on
  our Discord still works.
- Everything else in 9.2.6 works without the Flex Studio and Flex Preview
  downloads.

NEW: FLEX STUDIO (BETA) (FX & ADV RIGS)
- A new FLEX STUDIO (BETA) row in FX & ADV RIGS connects After Effects to Flex
  Studio, a new tracking and roto app for Mac (Apple silicon and Intel) and
  Windows.
- IN THE APP: planar tracking, roto shapes, the OBJECT BRUSH (click the person
  or object you want and it draws a roto shape around it; PROPAGATE follows it
  through the shot), plus Insert, Remove, Stabilize, Camera Solve and Lens
  calibration.
- HOW IT WORKS: select the footage layer, open FLEX STUDIO (BETA) and press
  OPEN. Flex Studio opens that layer over the comp's work area (up to 60
  seconds per shot). Track there and press APPLY TO AE: the track arrives in
  the panel by itself.
- ATTACH TO LAYER, one undo step each:
    TRANSFORM   position, rotation and scale follow the surface (onto a New
                Track Null by default)
    CORNER PIN  pins a layer onto the tracked surface
    STABILIZE   holds the surface still in the shot
    MASK        the roto shapes as After Effects masks, with feather and
                add / subtract
- MORE: CAMERA (a 3D camera from Flex Studio's camera solve), LENS (Optics
  Compensation from the lens calibration), WARP (the Flex Studio Warp effect:
  planar or mesh warp of a layer, with its own motion blur) and RENDER (brings
  a render made in Flex Studio in above the shot, at the right time).
- OPTIONS: keyframes on SMART (only the keys the motion needs), ALL, EVERY 2
  or EVERY 5; motion blur; stabilize zoom; mask feather.
- FIRST TIME: OPEN reads INSTALL FLEX STUDIO (BETA) with the download size. It
  installs the app and the Flex Studio Warp effect, then offers to restart
  After Effects. When a newer Flex Studio is out, a small UPDATE button appears
  next to OPEN (close Flex Studio first).
- If FFmpeg is not on your computer yet, Flex Studio downloads it once (about
  18 to 27 MB) the first time it opens a clip.
- Image sequences are not supported yet: render or import the shot as a movie
  file.
- THIS IS A BETA and the first public version. The After Effects exports
  (especially masks, camera, lens and the Warp effect) have had very little
  testing in real projects so far. Check each export before a final render,
  and tell us on our Discord when something does not land where you expect.

NEW: FORCE PREVIEW ENGINE INSTALLS FROM THE PANEL
- Force Preview Render no longer needs a zip from Discord. Without the Flex
  Preview engine, click the greyed Force Preview row and press DOWNLOAD &
  INSTALL: the panel picks the Mac or Windows build, shows its size and puts
  it in place.
- Then restart After Effects (the window offers RESTART AFTER EFFECTS) and the
  row is ready.
- When a newer engine is out, UPDATE ENGINE appears in the Force Preview
  window.
- Already installed the engine by hand from our Discord? It keeps working. The
  first time Flex installs a plug-in, it moves that copy into a new Flex
  Plugins folder, so After Effects never loads it twice.

NEW: FIVE MORE SPEECH ENGINES (AUTOCAPTIONS)
- The Transcription Model menu is now ranked by published accuracy: Qwen3-ASR,
  Moonshine, NVIDIA Canary, Whisper Large V3, Whisper Large V3 Turbo (still the
  default) and Whisper Medium, plus an INDIA group ranked by Hindi accuracy:
  AI4Bharat IndicConformer and SraVaani.
- The first time you use a new engine, it sets itself up (a few minutes) and
  downloads its model: Qwen3-ASR about 3.8 GB, Moonshine about 420 MB, Canary
  about 3.9 GB, IndicConformer 2.4 GB, SraVaani 0.9 GB. Setting up also
  installs the engine's own Python packages (up to about 1.5 GB more on disk).
  Canary, IndicConformer and SraVaani come from the Flex mirror: no Hugging
  Face account or token needed.
- The new engines need Python 3.10, 3.11, 3.12 or 3.13 on your computer
  (Whisper keeps using the Python it uses now). If there is none, AutoCaptions
  tells you; install Python 3.12 from python.org.
- Picking a model greys out the languages it cannot transcribe and moves the
  language to one it can. You can choose the folder the engines are stored in.
- Moonshine is the fastest for English; on a Mac it needs Apple silicon and
  macOS 15 or newer. For Hindi, IndicConformer and Qwen3-ASR gave the best
  results in our tests.
- So far the new engines have only been tested on an Apple silicon Mac. If one
  will not set up on your computer, tell us on our Discord; Whisper keeps
  working as before.

NEW: AUTOCAPTIONS SHOWS WHAT IT IS DOING
- The progress card names the model you picked ("Loading Whisper Large V3
  Turbo...") and shows what is really happening: the model download on first
  use with size, speed and time left, then the percent transcribed and how much
  of the audio is done ("3:00 of 6:00 of audio"). It no longer sits at
  "Loading Whisper... 30%".

NEW: UI SCALE (FLEX SETTINGS)
- Flex Settings > UI SCALE makes the whole panel smaller or bigger, like the
  display scale in Windows: one-click sizes from 75% to 150%, or anything from
  60% to 150% on the Panel Size slider. Text, buttons, windows, their close
  buttons and messages all scale together. 100% is the original size.
- This is new: if a drag or a click lands off target at another size, set it
  back to 100% and tell us on our Discord.

CHANGED: AUTOCAPTIONS ADDS NO HINTS TO THE SPEECH MODELS
- Every speech model now gets only your audio and the language: no example
  sentences, hints or custom words from Flex. Flex no longer adds its own
  example sentence to steer Whisper towards Roman Hinglish; that steering is
  paused for now.
- HINGLISH, or Hindi with Roman Script on: Whisper now writes the Hindi it
  hears and AutoCaptions turns it into Roman letters afterwards. These
  captions are likely to be less accurate than in 9.2.5: English words can
  come out spelled the Hindi way ("imej", "klik"), and a stray character from
  another alphabet can appear. Read them through before you render.
- English, Hindi in Devanagari and every other language are transcribed the
  same way as in 9.2.5.

CHANGED: LIGHTER ON AFTER EFFECTS
- Local AutoCaptions and local AI image generation now run at a lower
  priority, so After Effects stays more responsive while they work. Same
  results, and the same speed when nothing else is running.
- Looping preview videos in the panel pause while you work in After Effects
  and play again when you come back.
- On a Mac, the Downloader's "Optimizing video for AE" step now decodes most
  HEVC and VP9 videos on the Mac's video hardware, so it takes a lot less
  processor time. Full-range, interlaced and 10-bit videos keep the old way,
  so every file looks the same as before.
- Less background work in the AutoCaptions window, and on Windows while a
  caption job runs on the graphics card.

CHANGED: WINDOWS FILE AND FOLDER PICKERS
- On Windows, Flex's file and folder pickers now try the modern Explorer-style
  window instead of the old "Browse For Folder" tree. It is new and has not
  been tested on a Windows PC yet; whenever it cannot open, the old dialog
  appears instead, exactly as before.

FIXED
- AUTOCAPTIONS could stop with "Error loading Whisper model: Numba needs NumPy
  2.4 or less. Got NumPy 2.5." when installing Whisper or another Python
  package had pulled in a newer NumPy. It now installs a NumPy that Whisper
  accepts and runs the same job again (one time, needs internet). The repair
  is made to work on Windows as well, but so far it has only been tested on a
  Mac. Installing Whisper from the panel also picks a NumPy that works.
- ANCHOR GRID did nothing, or showed "ExtendScript Error", on layers whose
  Position or Anchor Point has keyframes (most caption and text animation
  presets) or whose Position is split into X and Y. It now moves the anchor on
  every keyframe, keeps rotated, scaled and 3D layers where they are, and names
  any layer it could not move, such as a locked one.
- LIBRARY FOLDERS: the Comp Saver library folder could go back to the default
  after a restart or after you changed or reset the presets folder, and a newly
  picked folder could be ignored in favour of the old one. Your choice now
  stays.
- Your Comp Saver Assets folder, Autosave location and Downloader save folder
  are now kept through updates too. If this update resets one of them, pick it
  once more; from now on it stays.

UNCHANGED
- Licensing, activation and every link work exactly as before.
- Every other tab and tool works exactly as in 9.2.5.
