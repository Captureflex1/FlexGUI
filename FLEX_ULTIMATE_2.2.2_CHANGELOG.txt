================================================================================
FLEX ULTIMATE FOR PREMIERE PRO - WHAT'S NEW
Version 2.2.2  -  October 2026
================================================================================

AUTO CUT now cuts and removes the silences in one step, SMART SYNCHRONISE
syncs your selected clips with a single click, ASSETS gets new template tabs
that keep arriving without an update, and AUTO CAPTIONS can run on an NVIDIA
graphics card. UN-NEST and NEST SEP,
rebuilt: nests with Motion, Opacity or effects now come apart, clips land in
order exactly where the nest was, your music and dialogue are never touched,
and both are much faster on long timelines. Plus new tracks that go on top,
and a few fixes.

BEFORE YOU START
- Install over your current version with your usual ZXP installer, then restart
  Premiere Pro once.
- Your quick effects, presets, settings and downloaded templates are kept.

NEW: MORE TEMPLATES IN ASSETS (ASSETS > PRESETS)
- THREE NEW TABS: TEXT EFFECTS (34 templates in Bounce, Glow, Gradient, Slides
  and More), COMMENT and FOLLOW. They are marked NEW.
- NEW TEMPLATES ARRIVE BY THEMSELVES. When we add templates, they appear in
  Assets with a NEW badge the next time you open Premiere Pro. No update to
  install.
- They download when you want them: Download, Download All, or double-click a
  tile. A tile shows its preview once its template is on your computer.

NEW: AUTO CAPTIONS ON YOUR NVIDIA GRAPHICS CARD (WINDOWS)
- Auto Captions ran on the processor even on PCs with an NVIDIA graphics
  card, because the standard Whisper install brings a processor-only engine.
  The Auto Captions engine setup now has an NVIDIA GPU row: Use GPU downloads
  the graphics-card engine once (about 2.5 GB), checks that it really runs on
  your card, and captions then run on the GPU. Same captions, much faster.
  Install / Fix Dependencies on such a PC does this by itself.
- The caption log says whether it ran on the GPU or the processor. If the card
  runs out of memory, the run finishes on the processor instead of stopping.
- Mac: unchanged, captions run on the processor.

CHANGED: ASSETS > PRESETS LOOK
- The download bar no longer squeezes its text into a thin column over the
  first templates. It sits above them, with the buttons underneath.
- One accent colour: the selected category, NEW badges, counts and the zoom
  slider follow the panel's green instead of red and blue.

CHANGED: AUTO CUT (SILENCE & CUT)
- CUT AND REMOVE IN ONE STEP. With REMOVE + RIPPLE, AUTO CUT used to cut the
  silences, then scan the timeline again gap by gap before ripple-deleting
  them, which took a long time on long clips. Now the removal follows the cuts
  straight away as one step ("CUTTING + REMOVING"), and the timeline is read
  once instead of once per gap. Ctrl+Z still takes the whole Auto Cut back in
  one press.
- REMOVE RETAKES gets the same speed-up.

CHANGED: SMART SYNCHRONISE (GENERAL TAB)
- ONE CLICK SYNCS. Select the clips to sync (for example the camera clip and
  the separately recorded audio) and click SMART SYNCHRONISE: Flex lines them
  up by their audio and opens the result as a new synced sequence. Your
  original sequence is not changed. The button shows its progress, and a
  second click cancels.
- SHIFT-CLICK FOR OPTIONS. Shift-click opens the options panel, redesigned:
  Sync by audio or Match properties, then the clips, the reference clip and
  the options in numbered steps, with Preview and Create at the bottom.

CHANGED: UN-NEST
- NESTS WITH MOTION, OPACITY OR EFFECTS. These used to be left intact with
  "Nest effects, motion or speed cannot be flattened faithfully". Now the
  nest's Motion, Opacity and Volume are folded into the clips, and its effects
  move to an adjustment layer above them (if your project has no Adjustment
  Layer item yet, Flex makes one first).
- STILL LEFT AS THEY ARE, WITH THE REASON: nests with a speed change or time
  remapping, multicam nests, nests on locked tracks, and nests whose audio has
  pan or effects.
- IN ORDER, IN PLACE. The clips come out exactly where the nest was, in their
  original order. If they would cover clips on the tracks above, Flex adds
  empty video tracks just above the nest instead of asking.
- SAFE. Nothing is written until every selected nest has been checked. If
  anything goes wrong, the timeline is put back exactly as it was.
- NO MORE "EXTRACTION FAILED" on nests with audio.
- FASTER on long timelines: on a 3,000-clip test timeline it reads the
  timeline about 24 times less.
- THE BUTTON stays in the same place: dimmed with "select a nest" until a
  nest is selected, then lit, with how many. It reads "working..." while it
  runs.

CHANGED: NEST SEP
- YOUR AUDIO IS SAFE. Music or dialogue on the audio tracks under the
  selected clips could be overwritten. It is no longer touched.
- New nests are named "<your sequence> Nest 01", "Nest 02"... in timeline
  order, and are selected when it finishes. The button shows what was made.
- If some clips cannot be nested, it says which, and keeps the rest.
- Much faster in big projects, and it can no longer pick up an older nest
  left over from a deleted sequence.

FIXED
- SILENCE REMOVER AND AUTO CAPTIONS on some Premiere Pro installs stopped with
  an error about JSON. They now work there too.
- MAC. The panel re-checked every colour swatch each time anything in it
  changed. It now does that only when you click a swatch.
- NEW TRACKS ON TOP. When Freeze Frame, Paste or an FX & ADV RIGS tool needed
  a new video track, Premiere inserted it as V2, pushing every track above it
  up one, and added an empty audio track. New tracks now go on top, with no
  extra audio track.
- AUTO CAPTIONS SETUP now uses the Python you picked in Flex Product Manager.
- LICENCE WATERMARK. The watermark read FLEX-ULT for everyone. It now shows
  part of your own key.

UNCHANGED
- Licensing, activation and every link work exactly as before.
- Every other tab and tool works exactly as in 2.2.0.
