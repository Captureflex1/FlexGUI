================================================================================
FLEX GUI PRO FOR AFTER EFFECTS - WHAT'S NEW
Version 9.2.4  -  October 2026
================================================================================

The biggest MAP RIGS update yet: new map looks, map data you can import and
draw, labels, routes, data maps and 3D, with no account and no API key, in a
new layout made for a tall panel that stays in sync with After Effects. Plus
Comp Saver sharing between Mac and Windows, Auto Captions on NVIDIA graphics
cards, a rebuilt 3D cursor for SaaS Effects, a steadier Un-Precomp and several
fixes.

BEFORE YOU START
- Install over your current version with your usual ZXP installer, then restart
  After Effects once.
- Your settings, theme, presets and saved expressions are kept.
- MAP RIGS needs no account and no API key. Map images and data come from free,
  open sources and download as you use them.

NEW: MAP RIGS (FX & ADV RIGS > MAP RIGS)
- NEW LAYOUT. The tools sit in a strip down the left side, with the map on top
  and the open tool's controls below it, made for a tall panel docked beside
  your comp. Hover an icon to see its name. Settings and help are at the
  bottom of the strip. The previous step-by-step layout is one switch away in
  MAP RIGS settings (Panel layout).
- AUTO-SYNC, ON BY DEFAULT. The panel works on the map you select in After
  Effects: select a map layer and the panel shows its view, without moving
  it. Move the panel map and the map in your comp follows. Stop for 5 seconds
  and its imagery, borders and labels refresh in the background while you
  keep working. Animated maps are followed but never changed. The dot at the
  bottom of the panel turns it off and on, Sync Now forces an update, and the
  5 seconds can be changed in settings.
- MAP LOOKS. A gallery with previews: Satellite, Earth Relief, Satellite +
  Borders, 25 street, dark and designed styles (Flex Basic, Midnight, Atlas
  Classic, Water Mask and more), plus hillshade and terrain looks. Edit a
  style's colours, or make one from your comp's colours, an image or an .ase
  swatch file.
- GEO CONTROLS. Latitude, Longitude and Zoom Level controls on the map, so you
  keyframe places instead of zoom and pan values. An existing zoom and pan
  animation can be converted. Also: an anchor null that rides the map, linked
  maps and a controller map, Refit after changing the comp size, and Duplicate
  and Remove that leave nothing behind.
- FINALIZE. Renders sharp imagery for every frame of the work area, so deep
  zooms stay crisp. Purge goes back to the base map.
- FEATURES. Import GeoJSON, KML/KMZ, GPX, TCX, CSV, zipped Shapefiles and
  TopoJSON, open ready-made datasets, or search OpenStreetMap, then draw the
  results as shape layers.
- LABELS AND PINS. Pin any layer to a place and it stays on it through zooms
  and turns. 6 starter label templates, labels for selected features, and
  automatic place names.
- SHAPES, ARROWS AND ROUTES. A style library for drawn features (marching
  dashes, line width that stays the same while zooming), arrows between two
  points, and routes along real roads by car, bike or on foot.
- DATA MAPS. Colour regions or size circles from a CSV file, animated over
  time, with a number readout and an optional live link to the CSV.
- 3D. Tilt the map with a camera, a 3D terrain model, 3D buildings, a
  hillshade layer, and a heightmap image for Helium or Plexus.
- PROJECT TOOLS (gear icon). Make Portable gathers the map files into one
  "Flex Map Files" folder and relinks them; Clean Up and Repair Maps keep
  projects tidy.
- SCRIPTS. Starter scripts, a script that runs when a project opens, and map
  functions you can use in your own ExtendScript.

NEW: SAAS EFFECTS (FX & ADV RIGS > SAAS EFFECTS)
- 3D CURSOR, REBUILT. 9 cursor looks in one layer: Arrow, Pointer, Auto, Grab,
  Grabbing, Text, Crosshair, Touch and Ring, switched from a Cursor Type menu
  in Effect Controls. Auto shows the arrow while it moves and the pointer
  over buttons. Choose 2D or 3D: in 3D the cursor tilts as it moves and lifts
  toward the camera. The path's curve stays editable, and an optional name
  tag follows the cursor, Figma-style.
- A NEW SAAS EFFECTS WINDOW. Pick a rig from a grid of icons; only that rig's
  settings open below, and the Create button stays in view at the bottom.

NEW: COMP SAVER SHARE (COMP SAVER > SHARE)
- SHARE BETWEEN MAC AND WINDOWS. A new Share tab packs all your categories,
  one category, or the comps you select into one .flexpack file. Packs made
  on a Mac open on Windows and the other way round, including file names that
  Windows does not allow.
- ANY AFTER EFFECTS. By default Flex adapts each saved comp to the After
  Effects that imports it, so comps saved in a newer version can be opened in
  an older one. You can also set a pack to open in an older After Effects
  (2025 down to CC 2018) for someone who does not have Flex.
- RECEIVE. Drop or open a .flexpack, old or new. It goes into the pack's own
  categories or one you choose, and never overwrites anything you have.

NEW: AUTO CAPTIONS ON YOUR NVIDIA GRAPHICS CARD (WINDOWS)
- Auto Captions ran on the processor even on PCs with an NVIDIA graphics
  card, because the standard Whisper install brings a processor-only engine.
  Auto Captions setup now has an NVIDIA GPU row: Use GPU downloads the
  graphics-card engine once (about 2.5 GB), checks that it really runs on your
  card, and captions then run on the GPU. Same captions, much faster.
  Installing Whisper on such a PC does this by itself.
- The caption log says whether it ran on the GPU or the processor. If the card
  runs out of memory, the run finishes on the processor instead of stopping.
- Mac: unchanged, captions run on the processor.

CHANGED
- COMP SAVER HEADER. Clearer icons, including a real share icon, in three
  groups, with the active button in solid green and a tooltip on each. Share
  now opens the Share tab with that category picked.
- UN-PRECOMP, STEADIER. Up to 12 selected precomps now come out in one step
  and one undo. Bigger selections run in batches of 12 (6 in Optimised Mode),
  and Esc stops between batches. A precomp that cannot be un-precomposed is
  skipped and named, instead of stopping the whole run. Precomps that were
  never moved, scaled or rotated come out faster.
- FLEX GRID REMOVED. The Flex Grid section is no longer in the Shape tab.

FIXED
- TOOLTIPS OFF MEANS OFF. With tooltips switched off in Settings, holding the
  pointer still for a few seconds still showed a small system tooltip on many
  buttons. Those are hidden too now, and come back when tooltips are on.
- MAC. The panel re-checked every colour swatch each time anything in it
  changed. It now does that only when you click a swatch.
- SAAS CURSOR SIZE. Scaling the cursor layer no longer breaks its path.
- SAAS RIGS MOVED IN TIME. A rig slid along the timeline kept its old timing:
  the cursor parked on its last target, and Stagger (after changing its
  duration), text typing and Carousel ran at the wrong moment. They now
  follow the layer wherever it starts.
- SAAS EASING. Position moves in the SaaS rigs were running at a constant
  speed in After Effects. Their eases now apply.
- MASK LAYER SPLITTER. Alt-click (split shape groups into layers) said there
  was nothing to split on shapes pasted from Figma or Illustrator, where
  everything sits inside one wrapper group. It now splits the groups inside
  that wrapper.
- AUTO CAPTIONS. Could stop with "No module named whisper" even with Whisper
  installed, because Flex checked one Python and then started another. It now
  starts the one it checked, and follows the Python you picked in Flex
  Product Manager.
- LICENCE WATERMARK. With a Combo key, the watermark read FLEX-COMBO for
  everyone. It now shows part of your own key.

UNCHANGED
- Licensing, activation and every link work exactly as before.
- Every other tab, tool and preset works exactly as in 9.2.3.
