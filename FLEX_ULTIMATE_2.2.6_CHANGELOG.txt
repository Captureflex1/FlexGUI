FLEX ULTIMATE FOR PREMIERE PRO - WHAT'S NEW
Version 2.2.6  -  October 2026

AUTO CAPTIONS now gives every speech model only your audio and the language:
no example sentences, hints or custom words from Flex. The "Numba needs
NumPy" error in Auto Captions and Caption Stack now repairs itself, and the
Motion Tracker in FX & ADV RIGS is
now called FLEX STUDIO.

BEFORE YOU START
- Install over your current version with your usual ZXP installer, then restart
  Premiere Pro once.
- Your quick effects, presets, settings and downloaded templates are kept.

CHANGED: NO FLEX HINTS TO THE SPEECH MODELS (AUTO CAPTIONS)
- Whisper and the other speech engines now get only your audio and the
  language you picked. Flex no longer adds its own Hindi and Hinglish hints or
  your custom words. The same goes for Remove Retakes and Caption Stack when
  they transcribe.
- ROMAN SCRIPT for Hindi: Whisper first writes Hindi in Hindi script, then the
  captions are converted to Roman letters. Some words follow the Hindi
  spelling (for example "bhee" instead of "bhi").
- Hinglish, and Hindi with Roman Script on, are likely to be less accurate
  than in 2.2.5, so check them before you export. English words can come out
  spelled the Hindi way ("veediyo" for "video"). Hindi in Hindi script and
  English captions came out the same as before in our tests.
- With the language on Auto and Roman Script on, a clip Whisper hears as Urdu
  now comes out in Urdu script. Pick Hindi or Hinglish to get Roman letters.
- REMOVE RETAKES on Hindi and Hinglish clips can find fewer retakes than in
  2.2.5, because words like "sorry" or "dobara" now come out in Hindi spelling
  (for example "soree", "dobaara"). Check the list before you delete.
- The CUSTOM WORDS field is hidden for now. Your saved list is kept as it is.

CHANGED: MOTION TRACKER IS NOW FLEX STUDIO (FX & ADV RIGS)
- The Motion Tracker row and page in FX & ADV RIGS are now called FLEX STUDIO.
  Tracking, Corner Pin, Paste, Stabilize and Smart keyframes work exactly as
  in 2.2.5.

FIXED
- AUTO CAPTIONS and CAPTION STACK could stop with "Numba needs NumPy 2.4 or
  less. Got NumPy 2.5" after another install updated NumPy. They now install a
  NumPy version Whisper accepts (once, needs internet) and carry on.
- Install / Fix Dependencies now installs that NumPy version from the start.
- The IndicConformer and SraVaani tooltips no longer say you need a Hugging
  Face account or token. Since 2.2.5 both download from the Flex mirror.

UNCHANGED
- Licensing, activation and every link work exactly as before.
- Every other tab and tool works exactly as in 2.2.5.
