================================================================================
FLEX GUI PRO FOR AFTER EFFECTS - WHAT'S NEW
Version 9.6.0  -  November 2026
================================================================================

AutoCaptions gets ULTIMATE's looks plus 21 new animated looks that come in as
real text layers you can edit word by word. FX & ADV RIGS gets an Assets tab
with ULTIMATE's template library, a Grid Transition and a new Shakes screen.
The Text tab, Morph and the Templates editor have a cleaner layout. Also fixes
for SPLIT, Comp Saver imports and section downloads.

BEFORE YOU START
- Windows: quit After Effects completely before you install.
- Install over your current version with your usual ZXP installer, then restart
  After Effects once.
- Your settings, theme and presets are kept.

NEW: 21 ANIMATED CAPTION LOOKS AS TEXT LAYERS (AUTOCAPTIONS > LOOK)
- New looks in the Real Estate group: Showcase, Open House, Skyline, Spark
  Count, Headline, Gilded, Chrome Line, Remix, Velvet, Springboard,
  Highlighter, Staircase, Boardroom, Marquee, Widescreen, Cockpit, Slide Deck,
  Neon Stack, Scrapbook, Stage Light and Orbit.
- Output: Text layers (new) puts every word on its own text layer in one comp,
  "Flex Captions - <look>", on top of your comp. Each word keeps its moves as
  keyframes, so you can change the text, font, colour or timing of any word.
  Glows, sparks and shapes sit on one clip under the words. Numbers count up
  through Source Text keyframes. Showcase, Open House and Skyline put your own
  footage inside the big words (a track matte).
- Output: Video clip makes one drawn clip, as before.
- A look whose fonts you do not have installs them for you (all free fonts).
  Restart After Effects once after that.

NEW: AUTOCAPTIONS LOOKS = ULTIMATE'S
- The LOOK card now shows the same templates as Flex ULTIMATE, and the
  AutoCaptions page opens at once.

NEW: FX & ADV RIGS > ASSETS
- ULTIMATE's template library in After Effects: titles, lower thirds,
  transitions, slideshows, social media, light leaks and more (1,886
  templates, 83 of them new: Colorful, Dynamic and Modern Liquid Slideshows;
  Cartoon, Liquid Motion, Neon, Seamless and Splash Liquid Transitions).
- Double-click a template: its After Effects project comes in, filed under a
  "Flex Assets" folder, and its comp drops into your comp at the playhead,
  fitted to the frame. Every layer, effect and control stays editable.
- Templates saved by a newer After Effects open without the version warning.
- Templates download the first time you use them.

NEW: GRID TRANSITION (FX & ADV RIGS > SAAS EFFECTS)
- A cut between two clips through a grid: cells flash in order (Diagonal,
  Random, Centre, Rows, Columns) and the second clip shows in each one.
  Pick FROM and TO (or USE SELECTED) and press CREATE TRANSITION. Everything
  is live on a "Grid Control" null.

NEW LAYOUTS
- Text tab, Morph and the Templates editor: two-pane layout, smaller
  controls. The text preview starts on its first frame, on plain black.
- Shakes: a list with a preview and an Apply button (double-click still
  applies).
- Force Preview: a smaller window and a drop-down mode.
- Tooltips are off by default. Turn them back on in Settings > Tooltips.

NEW: LOCAL MODE "FIX IT" (AUTOCAPTIONS)
- When Local mode will not run, FIX IT sets up its own private Python,
  Whisper and ffmpeg for Flex, with a repair for each failure it finds.
  Nothing else on your computer is changed.

FIXED
- Advanced+ SPLIT never split a layer (it always said "Move the playhead over
  the selected layers"). It now splits every selected layer under the
  playhead, following your "Create Split Layers Above Original" preference.
- Comp Saver: Undo after importing a saved comp said "Undo group mismatch".
  Fixed.
- Downloader: "Download a section" downloaded the whole video when the Flex
  Downloader app was installed. It now downloads the section.
- FX PRESETS: the List / Grid switch works again. The Shapes BETA notice is
  gone.

REMOVED
- The FLEX GRID tab. Flex Grid is being rebuilt as an effect of its own.

UNCHANGED
- Everything else, and your saved settings.
