================================================================================
FLEX ULTIMATE FOR PREMIERE PRO - WHAT'S NEW
Version 3.0.1  -  November 2026
================================================================================

Mouse & Macros gives your mouse buttons their own jobs in Premiere Pro, and the
AI tab becomes a chat that can also hand work to Claude Code or Antigravity.
Templates made in After Effects 2026 get a compatible copy on older Premiere.

BEFORE YOU START
- Install over your current version with your usual ZXP installer, then restart
  Premiere Pro once.
- Your settings, theme, presets and downloaded templates are kept.
- Mouse & Macros on Mac needs one permission: allow "Flex Mouse" in System
  Settings > Privacy & Security > Accessibility. The Allow button in the window
  takes you there.
- New interface text stays in English until the next translation pass.

NEW: MOUSE & MACROS (FLEX SETTINGS > MOUSE & MACROS)
- Give your mouse buttons their own jobs in Premiere Pro: the wheel click, back,
  forward and any extra button. Macs read up to 32 buttons. Windows reads five;
  extra buttons work there through the keys your mouse's own app assigns them.
- Click a button on the mouse picture and pick a keyboard shortcut, a Flex
  button, a script, a script file or a macro.
- Gesture button: hold it and move, scroll, or press another button.
- Macros: a row of shortcuts, Flex buttons, scripts and waits, run from a
  button, a gesture or their own hotkey.
- Ready to use: Back is Undo, Forward is Redo. Hold Back and scroll to zoom the
  timeline, or move to step frames and jump between edits.
- Buttons only do their job while Premiere Pro is in front.

NEW: THE AI TAB AS A CHAT
- A calm chat: your messages and the replies in one column, suggestions to
  start, and one box for text and the AI you want.
- Claude Code and Antigravity talk like a normal chat, on your own accounts. The
  panel installs them and signs you in without the Terminal, and they work in
  Premiere Pro through Flex. Pick the model from a menu.
- Gemini CLI and Any AI: type it roughly and Flex turns it into a clear prompt
  with your open sequence and selection, ready to paste.
- Flex AI, the built-in assistant on your credits, stays the default and now
  runs on Gemini 3.5. Older saved model choices that needed a key of your own
  now use Flex AI too.

NEW: FLEX AGENTS (AI TAB > AGENTS)
- Let Claude Code, Gemini CLI, Antigravity or any app that speaks MCP use
  ULTIMATE and Premiere Pro: read the open sequence, run changes and press Flex
  buttons.
- Off until you switch it on. One click adds it to each app, and only apps on
  this computer that hold its key can connect.

CHANGED
- Assets and Caption Stack: on Premiere Pro 2025 and earlier, templates made in
  After Effects 2026 now get a compatible copy before they are placed. They used
  to fail with "Unknown error while loading the Motion Graphics template". If
  one still will not load on your version, tell us on Discord.
- Assets: when Premiere refuses a template, the message now says so instead of
  "added at the playhead".

FIXED
- Downloads handed to Flex Downloader keep the section you chose with Download
  a section, instead of downloading the whole video.
- BG Remover no longer crashes on Mac. It now skips Core ML, which caused the
  crash, so very large images can take a little longer.
- AutoCaptions: cloud captions on Python 3.9 no longer say "Could not reach the
  Flex server". They use your Mac's certificates when Python has none.

UNCHANGED
- Your licence, credits and saved work. Everything not listed here works as it
  did in 3.0.0.
