================================================================================
FLEX ULTIMATE FOR PREMIERE PRO - WHAT'S NEW
Version 3.0.3  -  November 2026
================================================================================

AutoCaptions is one simple page now: pick the source, the engine and a look,
press Generate Captions, and every caption lands on the timeline as its own
editable template. Three timeline bugs are fixed and Audio Studio gets a tone
pad.

BEFORE YOU START
- Install over your current version with your usual ZXP installer, then restart
  Premiere Pro once.
- Your settings, theme, presets and downloaded templates are kept.
- New interface text stays in English until the next translation pass.

NEW: AUTOCAPTIONS ON ONE PAGE
- Source: Selected clip or Whole timeline. Press neither and it takes the
  selected clip, or asks about the whole timeline when nothing is selected. Two
  icons choose all of it or only the part between the sequence In and Out
  points. The file field and Browse are gone: the tab reads the timeline again
  every time you press Generate Captions.
- Engine: Cloud (Flex AI: nothing to install, the audio is uploaded, uses
  credits) or Local (Whisper on this computer: free, one-time setup). The model
  menu moved under More options.
- Look: the 20 Hook & Captions designs of the Assets tab, or plain Subtitles,
  with Size (Small, Medium, Large) and Position (Top, Middle, Low). A design's
  template is downloaded the first time you use it.
- Generate Captions places one template per caption on a track that is free
  there. Every word, font and colour stays editable in Essential Graphics.
- The captions are sized to the design itself: no more full-frame text, and
  the glow pack is gone.
- Caption Stack's page made way for the looks. Word Pop and the transcript are
  under More.

NEW: EDIT THE CAPTIONS IN THE EDITOR
- Select the generated captions on the timeline and open EDITOR: each caption
  is one line. Type, and the words go into that caption's template by
  themselves. Enter moves to the next line, Escape puts the old words back.
- All captions lists every generated caption without selecting them. The time
  beside a line moves the playhead to that caption.
- Same number of words: each word keeps its place in the design. More or fewer
  words: they are spread over the design again.

NEW: TONE PAD IN AUDIO STUDIO (PRESETS)
- Drag one dot: one way is dark to bright, the other is close to spacious. The
  corners are Dream, Stadium, Underwater and Broken radio, the centre is your
  sound as it is. Letting go plays the preview.
- Save a sound you like as your own preset (Mine).

CHANGED
- Assets: the side list of groups folds away on a second click.
- Downloader: when the Flex Downloader app is not on your computer, a card
  offers it. One press opens its download page.
- AI tab, Flex AI: a small ring in the message box shows how much of the chat
  it can still keep in mind. Rest the pointer on it for your usage and limits.

FIXED
- NEST SEP nested only the first selected clip. Every selected clip gets its
  own nest again.
- NEST SEP on the last clip of a timeline, when longer than 10 seconds, said
  the nest was made but could not be placed.
- UN-NEST failed when it needed a new track. Tools that add a track (UN-NEST,
  Paste Image, Timeline Shortcuts, FX & ADV RIGS) now add it on top instead of
  under the top track.
- L-CUT or J-CUT right after another split edit was refused with "the clip's
  speed is changed" although the speed was normal.
- FX & ADV RIGS said "engine did not load" when the panel was updated without
  restarting Premiere Pro. It now loads the new engine by itself.
- AutoCaptions with nothing selected asked for a media file instead of
  offering the whole timeline. It offers the whole timeline now, and Whole
  timeline on the new page uses the same path.

UNCHANGED
- Everything else: Auto Cut, Smart Sync, Motion Tracking, Mouse & Macros,
  themes and your saved settings.
