================================================================================
FLEX ULTIMATE FOR PREMIERE PRO - WHAT'S NEW
Version 2.2.9  -  October 2026
================================================================================

33 new ANIME THEMES join the Demon Slayer ones, the themes' mouse effects no
longer switch themselves off while the panel is busy, the Theme Selector is one
tidy dropdown, and confirm messages no longer hide behind Flex Settings.

BEFORE YOU START
- Install over your current version with your usual ZXP installer, then restart
  Premiere Pro once.
- Your settings, theme and presets are kept.

NEW: 33 ANIME THEMES
- Flex Settings > THEME SELECTOR > PANEL THEME now has one group per anime:
  Attack on Titan (Levi), Berserk (Guts), Black Clover (Asta), Bleach (Ichigo,
  Hitsugaya), Chainsaw Man (Denji, Reze), Code Geass (Lelouch), Dandadan
  (Okarun), Death Note (Kira), Dragon Ball (Goku), Frieren, Fullmetal
  Alchemist (Edward), Hunter x Hunter (Killua, Kurapika, Hisoka), Jujutsu
  Kaisen (Gojo, Sukuna), KonoSuba (Megumin), Mob Psycho 100 (Mob), My Hero
  Academia (Bakugo, Deku, Todoroki), Naruto (Itachi, Minato), One Piece
  (Luffy, Zoro, Law), One-Punch Man (Saitama), Re:Zero (Rem), Solo Leveling
  (Jinwoo), Spy x Family (Anya) and Tokyo Ghoul (Kaneki).
- Each character has their own look: what follows your mouse, the attack when
  you click a button, what happens while you hold the left button, and the
  blast after about 2.5 seconds of holding.
- Just as light as the Demon Slayer themes: the effects only run while you
  move or click. Switch AMBIENT EFFECTS off to keep only the colours.

CHANGED: THEME SELECTOR
- The Demon Slayer cards under PANEL THEME are gone, so Flex Settings is
  shorter. Pick every theme from the PANEL THEME dropdown; it opens on the
  theme you are using.
- Kurama and Baryon now sit in the Naruto group.

FIXED
- The themes' mouse effects (water, flames, lightning and the rest) could switch
  themselves off inside Premiere Pro: the panel's own busy moments, and a busy
  start, were taken for the theme lagging. Now, when your graphics card draws
  them (most computers), those moments only make the effects lighter for a
  moment.
- Picking a theme in Flex Settings did nothing you could see: the "Apply this
  theme anyway?" message opened hidden behind Flex Settings and only showed
  after you closed Flex Settings. The message now shows on top. The same goes
  for RESET ALL DATA and other confirm messages opened from a window.
- With ALIGN FOOTER switched off in Flex Settings, confirm messages could not
  show at all. They now always show.
- The PANEL THEME dropdown could keep showing the old theme's name after the
  theme changed. It now always shows the theme that is on.

UNCHANGED
- Licensing, activation and every link work exactly as before.
- The 11 Demon Slayer themes look exactly as in 2.2.8.
- Every other tab and tool works exactly as in 2.2.8.
