================================================================================
FLEX GUI PRO FOR AFTER EFFECTS - WHAT'S NEW
Version 9.5.5  -  November 2026
================================================================================

Flex AI in the AI tab now works in steps: it looks at your composition, does
the job with the panel's own tools, checks what it did and fixes it. Also a
Windows update fix and two AI tab fixes.

BEFORE YOU START
- Windows: quit After Effects completely before you install. The version you
  have now runs its Mouse & Macros helper from inside the panel folder, and
  Windows does not let an installer replace a program that is running.
- If the panel only shows a loading screen after an update, quit After
  Effects and install again with Flex Product Manager 2.1.2 or later.
- Install over your current version with your usual ZXP installer, then restart
  After Effects once.
- Your settings, theme and presets are kept.

NEW: FLEX AI WORKS IN STEPS (AI TAB)
- Flex AI used to write one script and run it without looking. Now it looks
  first: the composition, the selected layers, the frame. Then it makes a
  change with the panel's own tools, looks again to check it, and carries on
  until the job is done. Every step shows in the chat.
- Each step uses credits, like a reply. You can stop a run at any time.
- Needs you signed in to Flex AI in the AI tab. Image mode works as before.

FIXED
- Windows: the Mouse & Macros helper now runs from its own copy outside the
  panel folder, so it no longer stands in the way of the next update.
- AI tab: pressing Enter sent every message to the old Flex AI chat, whichever
  AI you had picked. Enter now sends to the AI you picked, like the send
  button.
- AI tab: pictures you attach now reach the AI you picked.
- Graph Editor > MINE: a right-click on a saved curve opened the rename box
  and the panel's own menu (Flex Settings, About Flex) on top of each other.
  Now only the rename box opens.

UNCHANGED
- Everything else, and your saved settings.
