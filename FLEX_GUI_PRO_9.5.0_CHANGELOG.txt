================================================================================
FLEX GUI PRO FOR AFTER EFFECTS - WHAT'S NEW
Version 9.5.0  -  November 2026
================================================================================

Edit Mode becomes a dock you build your panel with. Text Moments, named Shape
Tools and typed values in Graph Editor 2.0 join the panel. MAP RIGS now opens
in the MapRigs 2.0 app with an After Effects style workspace, and Flex Studio
reaches 1.0.0.

BEFORE YOU START
- Install over your current version with your usual ZXP installer, then restart
  After Effects once.
- Your settings, theme, presets, saved expressions and curves are kept.
- The map no longer runs inside the panel. The MAP RIGS tab now opens the
  MapRigs app and offers the download if it isn't on your computer.
- MapRigs and Flex Studio are not code-signed on Windows yet, so Windows may
  show a SmartScreen warning.
- New interface text stays in English until the next translation pass.

NEW: EDIT MODE AS A DOCK
- Settings > Layout Editor > Edit Mode. Arrange the panel on a dot grid with 59
  modules from all ten tabs.
- Drag a module and its neighbours move out of the way. Resize from any corner
  or edge, and the buttons reflow to fit.
- Add modules from a gallery with search and thumbnails.
- Keep three layouts on 1, 2 and 3, with Undo and Redo (5 steps) and RESET to go
  back to the original arrangement.
- For each module: lock, fill height, stick to bottom, frame, size, direction,
  columns, and show only on chosen tabs.
- For the whole dock: snap, grid density, spacing, corners, frames, starter
  layouts, and a layout code to copy and paste.
- A docked tool keeps updating while its tab is closed.
- MAP RIGS and Flex Glass Track Shape stay in their own tabs.

NEW: TEXT MOMENTS (Text > SaaS)
- Select Word: words build in, a selection box opens under a word and a menu
  pops up above it.
- Word Roll: the last word rolls through a list and lands in your accent colour
  with a glow.
- Tap Swap: a touch dot taps the text and it scrambles into new words.
- Circle Letters: letters scatter, hand-drawn circles draw around them, then
  they snap back.
- Fly In Scatter: letters fly in from scattered spots with blur.

NEW: SHAPE TOOLS (Shapes > SHAPE TOOLS)
- Create and Playground are a named list with a one-line description for each
  tool. A List / Grid switch remembers your choice.
- Stroke dashes and Trim path are one Stroke & trim section, with a live preview
  and readout on each card.

NEW: GRAPH EDITOR 2.0
- Click any parameter number and type it. Enter applies, Esc cancels, arrows
  nudge, Shift nudges by 10, Tab moves to the next one.
- Saved curves show in ejected windows, and a curve saved in one window appears
  in the others.
- DEF, BASE or MINE stays selected after a restart.

NEW: THEMES
- Six Awakened Eyes themes: Eclipse, Clockwork, Prism, Viper, Moth and Abyss.
  Original eye designs whose iris evolves through three stages.
- Holiday themes start by themselves only on the default look. Otherwise you get
  a Try it / No thanks notice, and turning one off is remembered for that
  holiday that year. Settings > Theme has a switch: Holiday themes on their day.
- New holiday looks: Christmas garland and snow (24 to 26 Dec), Halloween bats
  and embers, Friendship Day hearts (first Sunday of August).

NEW: MAPRIGS 2.0
- The MAP RIGS tab has OPEN MAP RIGS 2.0 (status, your comp and its maps, download
  or update) and IMPORT OPENED MAP RIGS, which links an app that is already open.
- One map per comp: if the comp has a Flex Map, Create uses it. LAUNCH ANOTHER
  INSTANCE allows a second one.
- After Effects style docking for 8 panels. Any panel can be its own window on a
  second screen. Workspaces: Default, Classic, Animation, Map Focus, Side by Side
  and 2 Screens. Save your own or lock one.
- An After Effects style timeline with keyframes on Anchor Point, Position,
  Scale, Rotation and Opacity, plus Z Position and X and Y Rotation for 3D.
- Parent & Link with a pick whip, Track Matte, one layer stack you drag to
  reorder, and A P S R T U as in After Effects.
- Flex Transform Null, a Maps layer, and layer effects: Drop Shadow, Tint, Fill,
  Glow, Gaussian Blur, Brightness & Contrast, Hue/Saturation.
- Graph Editor 2.0 with your GUI Pro curves.
- AUTOSYNC writes timeline key edits to the selected map in After Effects, in one
  undo step. What was already sent is undone in After Effects.
- 27 map effects: War, Front lines, Travel, Weather and Highlights, with 17 to 35
  controls each, handles on the map and keyframes for Position, Rotation, Size
  and Opacity. They use After Effects' built-in effects, with no sound. War
  effects show a notice first.
- A Flags tab with 270 flags and 51 US state flags: Fill Wave, Pole Flag or Badge.
- 236,830 towns and places as you type, offline (GeoNames). Deep search looks
  further online. Some towns have no outline in OpenStreetMap and stay a point.
- Every country in an A-Z list, a Borders point-of-view control (base map style
  lines don't follow it yet), and a map menu at the pointer (right-click or hold
  Tab).
- Text layers, 3D objects (GLB, glTF, OBJ, FBX, STL, PLY; the comp switches to
  Advanced 3D), drop or paste pictures, SVG, video, GeoJSON, KML and GPX, and
  Export Video (H.264 MP4 or PNG sequence).
- Known: fill and stroke colours and flag values have no keyframes yet.

NEW: FLEX STUDIO 1.0.0
- Out of beta. Your sessions, models and macOS permissions carry over, and the
  update removes the beta. Installing it in the same folder replaces the older
  standalone Flex STUDIO grading app.
- Studio Link: OPEN in FX & ADV RIGS puts the Flex STUDIO effect on the layer and
  links it. Every Save Grade then shows there, with no Apply press. It needs the
  Flex STUDIO effect 1.1.0: FX & ADV RIGS > FLEX STUDIO installs or updates it. A
  copy in the old folder is moved, which asks for your password once. An effect
  you graded by hand is left alone.
- Object Brush: SAM 2.1 Fast is the default on computers with a GPU path (about 8
  FPS against 0.9 before, measured on an Apple M5 with 24 GB). Hover lights up the
  object under the pointer. MODEL menu: SAM 2.1 Fast, SAM 2.1 and SAM 3 (about a 1
  GB download, needs 16 GB of RAM, recommended from 24 GB with a GPU).
- COLOR GRADING > ADJUSTMENT, laid out like Lumetri Color: Basic Correction,
  Creative, Curves, Color Wheels, HSL Secondary and Vignette.
- The Flex STUDIO effect renders on the GPU in After Effects on Mac (a 4K frame
  went from 180-414 ms to 86-96 ms on that same computer). Windows stays on the CPU.
- Frames read faster on Mac, about 1.75x for 4K HEVC.
- Your panel theme comes along when a session is opened from the panel.

FIXED
- AutoCaptions no longer stops with "The caption template file is missing" on
  Editable template output.
- The location privacy card no longer asks again on later launches.
- The old licence box no longer shows over the panel on a slow launch.
- Holiday themes no longer paint over the theme or colour you picked.
- Flex Studio: a pen shape no longer disappears when you press V or pick another
  tool. With 3 or more points it becomes a new layer.

UNCHANGED
- Your licence, credits and saved work. Everything not listed here works as it
  did in 9.3.0.
