================================================================================
FLEX ULTIMATE FOR PREMIERE PRO - WHAT'S NEW
Version 3.0.2  -  November 2026
================================================================================

A track made in Flex Studio now goes onto any clip with one press, the Assets
tab gets a sound library of your own folders, and the AI tab's agents can
listen, read a waveform and cut.

BEFORE YOU START
- Install over your current version with your usual ZXP installer, then restart
  Premiere Pro once.
- Your settings, theme, presets and downloaded templates are kept.
- Import tracking data works with the Flex Studio you already have.
- New interface text stays in English until the next translation pass.

NEW: IMPORT TRACKING DATA (FX & ADV RIGS > FLEX STUDIO)
- Track a surface in Flex Studio and press APPLY TO PREMIERE. Back in Premiere
  Pro select the clip that should follow it (a title, a logo, an image, another
  video) and press Import tracking data. One press: nothing to load first, no
  second page.
- Transform: the clip moves, turns and grows with the surface. It stays exactly
  where you put it on the frame you are looking at, and its Anchor Point is not
  moved: only Position, Rotation and Scale get keyframes.
- Corner Pin: the clip is pinned onto the surface by its four corners, wherever
  it was and however it was scaled or turned.
- Position: position only.
- Several selected clips get the track in the same press.
- Stabilize clip holds the tracked clip itself still. Remove takes the
  keyframes of the last import off again.
- If the tracked clip was moved or re-cut since it was tracked, the page says
  so instead of writing keyframes in the wrong place.

NEW: SFX IN THE ASSETS TAB (ASSETS > SFX)
- A PRESETS | SFX switch at the top of Assets. Add a folder of your own sounds
  and it becomes a library: the files stay where they are.
- Sounds are sorted into categories by their folder and name; takes of the same
  sound share one row.
- Rest the pointer on a sound to hear it (a click plays it too). Press + or
  double-click to put it on a free audio track at the playhead: it never lands
  on another clip.
- The tab starts empty: press Add a sound folder.

NEW IN THE AI TAB
- Flex Agents 1.2: an AI app working in Premiere Pro can now transcribe the
  selected clip on this computer (Whisper, no credits), look at a waveform to
  check a cut point, and cut through ULTIMATE's own silence engine. Long jobs
  report back instead of timing out.
- Review changes (on by default with Flex Agents): each change an agent wants
  to make waits for you: Run, Skip or Run all.
- The chat shows what an agent did as labelled rows: frames, audio, transcribe,
  waveform, cut.

CHANGED
- FlexBrowser: when the FlexBrowser app is not on your computer, its tab now
  reads DOWNLOAD FLEXBROWSER. One press downloads it, asks before installing
  (no password) and opens it. No separate installer to look for.
- A track sent from Flex Studio no longer needs the Motion Tracking page: the
  notice says where to import it. Motion Tracking > Paste tracking still works
  as before.

UNCHANGED
- Everything else: Auto Cut, AutoCaptions, Caption Stack, Audio Studio, Mouse &
  Macros, themes and your saved settings.
