================================================================================
FLEX GUI PRO FOR AFTER EFFECTS - WHAT'S NEW
Version 9.5.4  -  November 2026
================================================================================

AutoCaptions gets the same simple page as Flex ULTIMATE: source, engine, look,
one button. Flex Grid 2.0 is a grid that holds your layers and can be animated.

BEFORE YOU START
- Install over your current version with your usual ZXP installer, then restart
  After Effects once.
- Your settings, theme and presets are kept.
- New interface text stays in English until the next translation pass.

NEW: AUTOCAPTIONS ON ONE PAGE
- Three tabs: CAPTIONS, EDITOR (the old Style & Editor) and MORE (the local
  engine setup and the Whisper models).
- Source: Selected layer or Whole comp. Press neither and it takes the selected
  layer, or asks about the whole composition when nothing is selected. Three
  icons choose all of it, only the work area, or only where the layer sits in
  the composition. The file field, Detect, Scan Comp and Browse are off the
  page; "Use a media file instead" is under More options.
- Engine: Cloud (Flex AI: nothing to install, the audio is uploaded, uses
  credits) or Local (Whisper on this computer: free, one-time setup). The model
  menu, words per line, Roman script and Fill gaps are under More options.
- Look: the caption styles and layouts of the Editor, picked before the
  captions are made.
- Generate Captions transcribes and, with "Place in the comp when done" on,
  puts the captions in the composition with that look. Switch it off to style
  them in the Editor first and place them from there, as before.

NEW: FLEX GRID 2.0 (FX & ADVANCED RIGS > FLEX GRID)
- APPLY GRID: a grid on the comp, around the selected layer (it follows that
  layer), or with several selected layers filling its cells.
- Layers in cells: pictures, video, text, shapes and precomps are cropped to
  their cell and follow it. Fill, Fit or Keep. A layer can span several cells.
- Move a line or a crossing on the map and the comp follows.
- ANIMATE: DRAG (a cursor grabs crossings and pulls the grid) and the loops
  BREATHE, WAVE and SQUEEZE.
- Look: points, solid, dashed or dotted lines, width, colour, cell gap, rounded
  corners; visible in the render, as a guide only, or hidden.
- Everything it builds is plain After Effects: shape layers, masks and
  expressions. No plug-in to install.

CHANGED
- Downloader: when the Flex Downloader app is not on your computer, a card
  offers it. One press opens its download page.
- AI tab, Flex AI: a small ring in the message box shows how much of the chat
  it can still keep in mind. Rest the pointer on it for your usage and limits.

UNCHANGED
- AutoCaptions' Editor: templates, fonts, colours, motion, the word editor and
  Import to Timeline work as before.
- Everything else, and your saved settings.
