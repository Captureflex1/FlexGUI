================================================================================
FLEX GUI PRO FOR AFTER EFFECTS - WHAT'S NEW
Version 9.5.1  -  November 2026
================================================================================

FX Reveal brings eleven transitions to SaaS Effects, all driven by one slider.
The Particle Simulator turns a layer into dust that a null pushes around, free.
Mouse & Macros gives your mouse buttons their own jobs, and the AI tab becomes a
chat that can also hand work to Claude Code or Antigravity.

BEFORE YOU START
- Install over your current version with your usual ZXP installer, then restart
  After Effects once.
- Your settings, theme, presets, saved expressions and curves are kept.
- Mouse & Macros on Mac needs one permission: allow "Flex Mouse" in System
  Settings > Privacy & Security > Accessibility. The Allow button in the window
  takes you there.
- The Particle Simulator is a separate effect you install from its row. The
  first Flex effect you ever install asks for your Mac password once, and After
  Effects needs a restart to load it.
- New interface text stays in English until the next translation pass.

NEW: FX REVEAL (FX & ADV RIGS > SAAS EFFECTS)
- Eleven transitions for any layer: Tile Flip 3D, Domino 3D, Blinds 3D, Mosaic
  Push 3D, Shatter In, Pixel Dissolve, Glitch Slices, Vortex, Turbulent Flow,
  Plasma Burn and Lightning.
- Appears or Disappears, starting at the playhead. The whole transition runs off
  one "FX Reveal" slider with two keyframes: move or re-ease them to retime it.
- The 3D tiles take Tiles across, a Tilt and the side they come from. Energy
  colour fills the plasma rim and the lightning. Glow on or off.
- Each look plays as a live preview in the panel before you apply it.
- Built from After Effects' own effects, nothing to install. The last frame is
  your layer exactly as it was.

NEW: PARTICLE SIMULATOR (FX & ADV RIGS) - FREE
- Turns a layer into particles, about 500,000 at 1080p. A "Particle Control"
  null pushes, swirls and scatters them, and they settle back on their own.
  Scale the null to change the radius.
- Looks: Dust Push, Swirl, Stream, Explode, Magnet, Assemble and Jelly, with
  return time, bounce, dust glow, density, and 3D depth from brightness or AI
  depth.
- A native effect for Mac and Windows, installed and updated from the row.
  Free for every GUI Pro user.

NEW: MOUSE & MACROS (FLEX SETTINGS > MOUSE & MACROS)
- Give your mouse buttons their own jobs in After Effects: the wheel click, back,
  forward and any extra button. Macs read up to 32 buttons. Windows reads five;
  extra buttons work there through the keys your mouse's own app assigns them.
- Click a button on the mouse picture and pick a keyboard shortcut, a Flex
  button, a script, an After Effects menu command, a script file or a macro.
- Gesture button: hold it and move, scroll, or press another button.
- Macros: a row of shortcuts, Flex buttons, scripts, menu commands and waits,
  run from a button, a gesture or their own hotkey.
- Ready to use: Back is Undo, Forward is Redo. Hold Back and scroll to zoom the
  timeline, or move to step frames and keyframes.
- Buttons only do their job while After Effects is in front.

NEW: THE AI TAB AS A CHAT
- A calm chat: your messages and the replies in one column, suggestions to
  start, and one box for text, images and the AI you want.
- Claude Code and Antigravity talk like a normal chat, on your own accounts. The
  panel installs them and signs you in without the Terminal, and they work in
  After Effects through Flex. Pick the model from a menu.
- Gemini CLI and Any AI: type it roughly and Flex turns it into a clear prompt
  with your open comp and selection, ready to paste.
- Flex AI, the built-in assistant on your credits, stays the default and now
  runs on Gemini 3.5.

NEW: FLEX AGENTS (AI TAB > AGENTS)
- Let Claude Code, Gemini CLI, Antigravity or any app that speaks MCP use GUI Pro
  and After Effects: read the open comp, run changes as one undo step, press Flex
  buttons, apply your saved expressions and eases, and check the result with a
  snapshot.
- Off until you switch it on. One click adds it to each app, and only apps on
  this computer that hold its key can connect.

CHANGED: FORCE PREVIEW
- RECOMMENDED FOR THIS COMPUTER: sizes After Effects' disk cache from your free
  space (more than 200 GB free: half of it, up to 2 TB) and tests Multi-Frame
  Rendering on your own comp, turning it on only where it is faster. APPLY
  RECOMMENDED does both. The disk cache size applies from the next start.
- Move the playhead (on by default): the playhead hops across the frames that
  are ready, then goes back where it was.
- Memory Auto picks a safe amount for your RAM, with a warning above it.

FIXED
- The panel no longer freezes after two pastes in a row.
- Mac: with the panel focused, Space plays the preview, Cmd+Z and Shift+Cmd+Z
  undo and redo in After Effects, and Cmd+V over PASTE IMAGE pastes your image.
- Command Mode: commands with quotes or other special characters in a name or
  text (shapes, text layers, rename, effects) no longer fail.
- BG Remover no longer crashes on Mac. It now skips Core ML, which caused the
  crash, so very large images can take a little longer.
- Halftone Reveal no longer shows "This project contains expression errors",
  and the transition runs.
- Text Properties: the colour swatches open the colour picker again.
- AutoCaptions: cloud captions on Python 3.9 no longer say "Could not reach the
  Flex server". They use your Mac's certificates when Python has none.
- Force Preview no longer says "The comp is no longer in the project" after you
  switch comps.

UNCHANGED
- Your licence, credits and saved work. Everything not listed here works as it
  did in 9.5.0.
