================================================================================
FLEX GUI PRO FOR AFTER EFFECTS - WHAT'S NEW
Version 9.5.2  -  November 2026
================================================================================

Force Preview now checks its own work. After the render it goes through the
cache, renders again any frame that dropped out, and tells you plainly when
After Effects cannot hold them all. And the stuttering "zombie" sound of a
preview that is not in real time is gone: After Effects mutes it instead.

BEFORE YOU START
- Install over your current version with your usual ZXP installer, then restart
  After Effects once.
- Update the engine too. Open FX & ADV RIGS > FORCE PREVIEW RENDER: the window
  says "Flex Preview 1.4.0 is out". Press UPDATE ENGINE, then restart After
  Effects. The cache check needs the new engine. No zombie audio and the disk
  space warning work without it.
- Your settings, theme, presets, saved expressions and curves are kept.
- The new text is in English until the next translation pass.

NEW: NO ZOMBIE AUDIO (FORCE PREVIEW)
- When a preview cannot keep up, After Effects plays the sound in broken,
  stuttering pieces. With No zombie audio on, After Effects stays silent until
  the preview is in real time, then plays clean sound.
- On by default. It switches on the first time you press Render preview or
  APPLY RECOMMENDED, and a note tells you once.
- It is After Effects' own setting, "Mute Audio When Preview Is Not Real-time"
  (Preferences > Previews), so it also applies when you press Space without
  Force Preview. Switch it off in the Force Preview window: No zombie audio.

NEW: FORCE PREVIEW CHECKS THE CACHE (ENGINE 1.4.0)
- After the last frame, Force Preview goes through the range once more. Frames
  that dropped out of After Effects' cache during the render are rendered
  again, then it checks a second time before it says READY TO PLAY.
- This fixes the case where it said done, but Space still stuttered on the
  first seconds: those frames had been pushed out while the rest rendered.
- When After Effects cannot hold every frame, the window says about how many do
  not fit and why (memory full, or the cache drive too full), instead of saying
  everything is ready.
- If your computer runs out of memory during a render, Force Preview stops,
  keeps what it rendered and tells you.

NEW: DISK SPACE WARNING (RECOMMENDED FOR THIS COMPUTER)
- After Effects stops saving preview frames to disk when the cache drive is
  nearly full (10 GB free by default). From then on, frames that leave memory
  are lost and the preview stutters.
- The Recommended card now warns you when you are within 10 GB of that limit
  and shows how much is free. Free up space, or empty the cache with Edit >
  Purge > All Memory & Disk Cache.

GOOD TO KNOW
- With little free memory (several Adobe apps open), a cached preview can still
  drop below real time for a moment. With No zombie audio on you hear a short
  silence instead of stutter.
- For long comps, render the part you are reviewing: set the work area with B
  and N, or lower the resolution to Half.

UNCHANGED
- Your licence, credits and saved work. Everything not listed here works as it
  did in 9.5.1.
