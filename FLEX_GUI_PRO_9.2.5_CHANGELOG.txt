FLEX GUI PRO FOR AFTER EFFECTS - WHAT'S NEW
Version 9.2.5  -  October 2026
================================================================================

Force Preview Render: one click fills After Effects' green bar for the whole
work area and tells you how long it will take, so the comp can play back in
real time. It can even render while you are away from the computer. Plus a
quieter Autosave that keeps saving without popping up a message every few
minutes.

BEFORE YOU START
- Install over your current version with your usual ZXP installer, then restart
  After Effects once.
- Your settings, theme, presets and saved expressions are kept.
- Force Preview Render needs the free Flex Preview engine, a separate download
  from our Discord (one zip for Mac and Windows):
    Mac: drag FlexPreview.bundle into
         /Library/Application Support/Adobe/Common/Plug-ins/7.0/MediaCore
    Windows: double-click Install Flex Preview.bat and allow it.
  Then restart After Effects. Everything else in 9.2.5 works without it.

NEW: FORCE PREVIEW RENDER (FX & ADV RIGS)
- A new row under Comp Resizer. One click renders every frame of the active
  comp's work area (or the whole comp) into After Effects' preview cache, the
  green bar. You see frames done, speed and time left while it works, and you
  can keep working between frames. Play When Done starts playback the moment
  every frame is ready.
- TIME BEFORE YOU START. After a comp has been rendered once, the window shows
  how long the next render will take before you press the button.
- SPEED. SMOOTH keeps After Effects fully responsive (slowest), FAST works in
  short bursts so you can keep working with small pauses, MAX is the fastest
  (After Effects pauses while it works).
- RESOLUTION. Keep the comp's resolution, or switch it to Full, Half (about
  4x faster) or Quarter (about 16x faster) for the render.
- MEMORY. A slider sets how much memory the preview may take. Only the frames
  that fit are rendered, so none of them drop out of the green bar again. When
  they do not all fit, it tells you whether Half or Quarter resolution would
  fit them, with a button to switch.
- AUTO PREVIEW. Step away from the computer (no keyboard or mouse for 30
  seconds, 1, 2 or 5 minutes) and Flex renders the active comp's work area for
  you: "YO! You stepped away, so I'm rendering your preview". The moment you
  are back it stops, says how much it finished, and everything it rendered
  stays ready to play. On by default; switch it off in the Force Preview
  window.
- SHORTCUT. With the engine installed, Composition > Flex: Force Preview Render
  works without opening the panel. Give it a key in Edit > Keyboard Shortcuts.
- Without the engine the row is greyed out and links to our Discord, where the
  engine is.
- If a render finishes but the green bar stays empty, open Advanced in the
  Force Preview window and turn on Hand Frames To The Cache Myself.

CHANGED
- AUTOSAVE, QUIETER. Autosave still saves on the same timer, with the same
  backup versions, but the two messages it showed on every save ("Autosaving
  project..." and "Autosave complete!") are gone. The Autosave pill at the top
  of the panel flashes SAVED for a moment instead, or SKIPPED - RQ when the
  Render Queue is busy. A save that really fails still shows its message.
- While Auto-Save is on, the pill no longer turns red for the last 30 seconds
  of the countdown, because there is nothing you need to do: it saves by
  itself. With Auto-Save off, the red reminder to save stays.
- Prefer the old messages? Alt+Shift+Click the Autosave pill to open Autosave
  Settings and turn off Quiet Mode > Hide Pop-ups.
- A project that has never been saved still opens the Save dialog when
  Autosave comes round, because that is the only way it can be saved.

NEW: HALFTONE REVEAL (SAAS EFFECTS > DOT REVEAL)
- Reveal or hide a layer through a moving dot or pixel edge, using native
  After Effects effects. Pick a centre, left, right, top or bottom reveal.
- Control duration, easing, edge width, dot/pixel size and softness. Optional
  pixelation, editable controls and a live preview in the panel.

NEW: ADVANCED+ AND PROPERTY CLIPBOARD (FLEX SETTINGS)
- Turn on Advanced+ below Layout Editor for compact creation and editing
  tiles beside the anchor grid: Null, Adjustment, Solid, Camera, Light, Text,
  Shape, Cut, Split, Fit, Center and Duplicate.
- Create a shape sized to the selected layer, or an empty shape when no layer
  is selected. The layout adapts to narrow panels.
- Four Property Clipboard slots copy and paste transforms, effects, blending,
  3D and motion blur. Keyframed properties receive a key at the playhead.

FIXED
- SPLIT handles eligible layers individually. Locked layers and layers outside
  the playhead are skipped, so one unsplittable layer no longer stops the rest.
- Long tooltips wrap and stay inside the panel instead of running off-screen.

UNCHANGED
- Licensing, activation and every link work exactly as before.
- The Flex Preview engine is not inside this ZXP; it stays a separate
  download from our Discord.
