================================================================================
FLEX ULTIMATE FOR PREMIERE PRO - WHAT'S NEW
Version 3.0.4  -  November 2026
================================================================================

Flex AI in the AI tab now works in steps, Word Pop builds real word clips you
can edit, and the Undo buttons of Auto Cut, Cleanup and Retakes really bring
the cut back. Also fixes for the 4-colour gradient, Nest Deck, right-click
menus and Windows updates.

BEFORE YOU START
- Windows: quit Premiere Pro completely before you install. The version you
  have now runs its Mouse & Macros helper from inside the panel folder, and
  Windows does not let an installer replace a program that is running.
- If the panel only shows a loading screen after an update, quit Premiere Pro
  and install again with Flex Product Manager 2.1.2 or later.
- Install over your current version with your usual ZXP installer, then restart
  Premiere Pro once.
- Your settings, theme, presets and downloaded templates are kept.

NEW: FLEX AI WORKS IN STEPS (AI TAB)
- Flex AI used to write one script and run it without looking. Now it looks
  first: the sequence, the selected clips, the frame in the Program Monitor.
  Then it makes a change with the panel's own tools, looks again to check it,
  and carries on until the job is done. Every step shows in the chat.
- Each step uses credits, like a reply. You can stop a run at any time.
- When Flex AI looks at a frame, Premiere Pro shows its own short "Exported
  frame" notice in the corner. That is expected.
- Needs you signed in to Flex AI in the AI tab. Image mode works as before.

NEW: WORD POP MAKES REAL WORD CLIPS (AUTOCAPTIONS > MORE > WORD POP)
- On Premiere Pro 2026, Word Pop placed clips that could not take a word.
  Now it places one clip per word: bold white with a dark edge, low in the
  frame, popping in. The clips are named "Flex Word 1", "Flex Word 2" and so
  on, and every word stays editable in Essential Graphics.
- Target Track 0 puts the words on a free track above everything (a new one
  on top when there is none).
- Diagnostics places one test word at the playhead and tells you whether it
  worked.

FIXED
- UNDO LAST AUTO CUT and UNDO LAST CLEANUP never undid anything in Premiere
  Pro (they stopped with an error). They now take Premiere's own history back
  to just before the cut. This works for cuts made since the panel was opened.
  If you made other changes after the cut, it asks first.
- Retakes: Ctrl+Z in Premiere brought a removed retake back only partly. The
  Retakes footer now has an Undo button that brings the whole removal back.
- Colors tab: every 4-colour gradient failed with "Premiere accepted the
  effect but did not report it back on the clip". It lands again.
- UN-NEST now asks before it leaves out an effect that this Premiere Pro does
  not have. FX & ADV RIGS and Smart Sync no longer report an effect as added
  when Premiere did not add it.
- Nest Deck: double-click a deck to rename it works again.
- A right-click on a saved curve (Graph Editor > MINE), an Assets template or
  a Nest Deck opened the tool's menu and the panel's own menu on top of each
  other. Now only the tool's menu opens.
- AutoCaptions Editor: "All captions" fell back to an empty list when the
  selection changed (for example with Selection Follows Playhead on). It now
  stays until you press "Only selected".
- Windows: the Mouse & Macros helper now runs from its own copy outside the
  panel folder, so it no longer stands in the way of the next update.

UNCHANGED
- Everything else: AutoCaptions, Smart Sync, Motion Tracking, Audio Studio,
  themes and your saved settings.
