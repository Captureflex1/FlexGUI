================================================================================
FLEX ULTIMATE FOR PREMIERE PRO - WHAT'S NEW
Version 2.2.8  -  October 2026
================================================================================

CAPTION STACK captions are now EDITABLE Motion Graphics templates with a real
editor, there is a new AUDIO STUDIO and MARK AUDIO BEATS, the panel speaks 132
LANGUAGES, UI SCALE arrives in ULTIMATE, and there are new DEMON SLAYER THEMES.

BEFORE YOU START
- Install over your current version with your usual ZXP installer, then restart
  Premiere Pro once.
- Your settings, theme and presets are kept.

NEW: CAPTION STACK - EDITABLE CAPTIONS (FX & ADV RIGS > CAPTION STACK)
- Captions are now placed as editable Motion Graphics templates (Output >
  EDITABLE MOGRT, the new default in Premiere Pro 2026). After placing,
  change any word, font, colour, size, position, glow, outline, box,
  animation or timing in Premiere's Essential Graphics panel.
- Output > IMAGES still places every word as a picture with the exact look,
  if you prefer that.
- The templates come with the panel. Nothing to download.

NEW: CAPTION STACK EDITOR
- The preview now shows the real frame of your video under each caption.
- Drag a caption to move it, use the corner to resize and the top dot to
  turn it, double-click a word to edit it. WHOLE CAPTION moves every word
  together, ONE WORD moves a single word.
- Choose whether a change applies to every caption or this caption only, and
  save your layouts to reuse them.
- 10 new GLOW looks: Big Wide, Bounce Lime, Candy, Horror, Hook, Typewriter,
  Editorial, Sunset, Slide Yellow and Superb.

NEW: AUDIO STUDIO (FX & ADV RIGS > AUDIO STUDIO)
- Select an audio clip, pick an effect and press APPLY. Everything runs on
  your computer.
- 30 one-click presets: Underwater, Bass Boost, Room / Hall / Cathedral /
  Plate reverb, Echo, Telephone, Radio, Megaphone, Robot, Chipmunk, Deep
  Voice, Nightcore, Slowed + Reverb, 8D Audio, Lo-fi, Reverse and more. Stack
  as many effects as you like, reorder them or switch one off.
- Reverb keeps its tail: the sound rings out past the end of the clip, and it
  is placed so nothing on your timeline gets overwritten.
- Tools: Noise Remover, Voice Enhance, Loudness (-14 / -16 / -23 LUFS),
  De-esser, De-click, De-clip, Hum Remover, Compressor, Limiter, EQ, Speed
  and Pitch, Noise Gate, Extract Audio.
- STEMS MAKER splits a song into vocals and music (or 4 stems). It downloads
  its model the first time and asks you first.
- PREVIEW lets you hear about 8 seconds with an Original / Processed switch.
  The result goes on a free audio track and your original clip is muted,
  never deleted.

NEW: MARK AUDIO BEATS (FX & ADV RIGS > MARK AUDIO BEATS)
- Select a music clip on the timeline and press MARK BEATS: Flex finds the
  beats and adds sequence markers on them, so your cuts snap right to the
  music.
- Pick ALL hits, KICKS or SNARES, set the sensitivity, and mark every beat or
  every 2nd or 4th beat.
- REMOVE BEAT MARKERS clears only the markers Flex added. Runs on your
  computer.

NEW: THE PANEL IN 132 LANGUAGES
- Settings > LANGUAGE now lists 132 languages, with a search box. Tabs,
  buttons, tooltips and messages all switch. The "what's new" popup is
  translated too, from this release on.

NEW: UI SCALE
- Settings > UI SCALE makes the whole panel bigger or smaller, like in
  Flex GUI Pro. Every window fits at every size.

NEW: DEMON SLAYER THEMES
- Settings > THEME SELECTOR has a new DEMON SLAYER group with 11 themes:
  Giyu, Hinokami Kagura, Shinobu, Mitsuri, Rengoku, Zenitsu, Nezuko,
  Muichiro, Inosuke, Tengen and Kokushibo.
- Move the mouse over the panel and the theme's element follows it: water,
  flames, lightning, butterflies, petals, mist and more. When the mouse
  stops, it fades away.
- Click a button and that character's sword slashes it. Keep the left button
  held and it keeps slashing; hold it for about 2.5 seconds and it explodes.
- Made to stay light: the effects only run while you move or click, and they
  get lighter by themselves on slower computers. Switch AMBIENT EFFECTS off
  in the Theme Selector to keep only the colours.

CHANGED: FLAT BUTTONS IN EVERY THEME
- Active and main buttons in the colour themes are one flat colour with a soft
  glow instead of a shaded gradient.

FIXED
- The licence card and the "what's new" popup could open on top of each
  other. They now never overlap, and the UPDATE badge no longer covers the
  buttons at the top.
- Windows: Auto Captions could say "THE NVIDIA DRIVER IS TOO OLD FOR GPU
  CAPTIONS" on new cards and drivers (RTX 40 and 50 series). It now reads the
  driver's CUDA support correctly and uses your GPU.

UNCHANGED
- Licensing, activation and every link work exactly as before.
- Every other tab and tool works exactly as in 2.2.7.
