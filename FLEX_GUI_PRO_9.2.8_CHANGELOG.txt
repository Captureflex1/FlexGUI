================================================================================
FLEX GUI PRO FOR AFTER EFFECTS - WHAT'S NEW
Version 9.2.8  -  October 2026
================================================================================

The panel now speaks 132 LANGUAGES, there are new DEMON SLAYER THEMES with a
sword that slashes the buttons you press, and a few layout and Windows fixes.

BEFORE YOU START
- Install over your current version with your usual ZXP installer, then restart
  After Effects once.
- Your settings, theme, presets and saved expressions are kept.

NEW: THE PANEL IN 132 LANGUAGES
- Flex Settings > LANGUAGE now lists 132 languages, with a search box. Pick
  one and the whole panel switches: tabs, buttons, tooltips, messages.
- Every language was translated by AI from the English panel, not word by
  word. Names you see in your project (layer names, effect names) stay in
  English so they still match After Effects.
- The "what's new" popup is translated too, from this release on.

NEW: DEMON SLAYER THEMES
- Flex Settings > THEME SELECTOR has a new DEMON SLAYER group with 11 themes:
  Giyu, Hinokami Kagura, Shinobu, Mitsuri, Rengoku, Zenitsu, Nezuko,
  Muichiro, Inosuke, Tengen and Kokushibo.
- Move the mouse over the panel and the theme's element follows it: water,
  flames, lightning, butterflies, petals, mist and more. When the mouse
  stops, it fades away.
- Click a button and that character's sword slashes it. Keep the left button
  held and it keeps slashing; hold it for about 2.5 seconds and it explodes.
- Made to stay light: the effects only run while you move or click, and they
  get lighter by themselves on slower computers. Switch AMBIENT EFFECTS off
  in the Theme Selector to keep only the colours.

CHANGED: FLAT BUTTONS IN EVERY THEME
- Active and main buttons in the colour themes no longer use a shaded
  top-to-bottom gradient. They are one flat colour with a soft glow, so they
  look cleaner and read better.

FIXED
- The licence card and the "what's new" popup could open on top of each
  other. They now never overlap, and the UPDATE badge no longer covers the
  buttons at the top of the panel.
- At UI SCALE 125% and 150%, some windows (like Flex Settings) could grow
  taller than the panel, so their close button went out of view. Every window
  now fits at every size.
- Windows: AutoCaptions could say "THE NVIDIA DRIVER IS TOO OLD FOR GPU
  CAPTIONS" on new cards and drivers (RTX 40 and 50 series). It now reads the
  driver's CUDA support correctly and uses your GPU.

UNCHANGED
- Licensing, activation and every link work exactly as before.
- Every other tab and tool works exactly as in 9.2.7.
