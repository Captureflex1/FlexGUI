================================================================================
FLEX GUI PRO FOR AFTER EFFECTS - WHAT'S NEW
Version 9.3.0  -  October 2026
================================================================================

MAP RIGS 2.0 arrives with the Map desk, Quick looks and Flex Maps, and its
extra tools finally load inside After Effects. AutoCaptions gets your script,
Gemini 3.5 Transcribe, a whole-comp option, transcript reuse and an editable
template look. Flex Studio (Beta) now downloads first and asks before it
installs, and theme effects show again on many Windows PCs.

BEFORE YOU START
- Install over your current version with your usual ZXP installer, then restart
  After Effects once.
- Your settings, theme, presets and saved expressions are kept, with two
  exceptions: AutoCaptions switches to Gemini 3.5 Transcribe once (see below;
  you can pick your old engine again), and if Flex turned Optimised Mode on by
  itself, it is checked once more and may switch back off (see Fixed).

NEW: MAP RIGS 2.0
- The Map desk, now the default layout: the map with its navigation strip,
  search, a Features list and a status line in one view, and Create map in
  the header (Finalize joins it once you pick a map). The older layouts are
  still there: ⋯ > Preferences > Panel layout (Tool strip or Classic steps),
  or ⋯ > Switch to Classic steps.
- Search places and coordinates offline as you type (countries and major
  cities); press Enter to search OpenStreetMap too.
- Quick looks: pick one, fill in a short form (a country, a place or your
  stops) and press Make it. The looks are Country Pop, Space Dive (from a
  turning globe down onto the map), Trip Route, Range Rings, Strike Arc,
  Advance Arrow, Hatch Zone and Explainer Desk. Most are built into the map
  you are editing, so pick or create a map first. Place boxes work offline: a
  country, about 60 big cities, the place picked on the map, or a lat, lon.
- Undo, or Remove this look right after you make it, takes the look's layers
  away. Space Dive leaves its dive map (and globe), Trip Route its camera keys
  and Explainer Desk its map style and push-in: remove those by hand if you
  don't want them.
- Labels: Stick to Map pins a label to its place, and Clean labels thins them
  out (Few, Balanced or Many). Always Sharp (Quick looks > Finish) marks the
  soft stretches on the map layer and fixes them with Smart Finalize, one
  image per zoom level; on the Finalize card the same tools are Check
  sharpness and Smart (one image per level).
- Free map styles, no key needed. Satellite now comes from Sentinel-2 (EOX):
  sharp down to about city level, but softer than the old Esri satellite for
  street-level close-ups, so use a vector style such as Flex Detailed up
  close. Esri, CARTO, Mapbox, MapTiler and Stadia can't be added as custom
  sources.
- The map's bearing now turns the same way in After Effects as in the panel.
- Open in Flex Maps (Map desk header, or ⋯ > Open in Flex Maps) opens MAP RIGS
  in a bigger window, the Flex Maps (Beta) app, linked to After Effects:
  select your map layer in After Effects to drive it from there, and keep
  Flex GUI Pro open while you use it. If the app is not on your computer, it
  downloads it first and asks before it installs.
- Known: with both a bearing and a pan set, the view can drift off the comp
  centre, so keep the bearing at 0 while panning until a fix lands. A map made
  with a plain Create arrives unturned (bearing 0) until you move it once
  with live sync on, and fly-throughs made before 9.3.0 still turn the old
  way.
- MAP RIGS 2.0 is new: if the Map desk doesn't suit your panel, Tool strip and
  Classic steps are one setting away. If something looks wrong in your comp,
  please tell us on Discord.

FIXED: MAP RIGS' EXTRA TOOLS NOW LOAD
- Since 9.2.4, MAP RIGS' extra tools (live sync, map styles, labels, 3D,
  Finalize and the rest) did not load inside After Effects: the panel looked
  for them with a path After Effects does not give out. They load now.

NEW: AUTOCAPTIONS - GEMINI 3.5 TRANSCRIBE
- A cloud engine, now the recommended one, selected once for everyone. It
  uploads your audio to Flex AI, uses Flex AI credits and needs internet.
- To keep captioning on your computer for free, pick your Whisper model again
  under Transcription Model; your choice is then kept.
- Translate To needs Whisper (Large V3 or Medium): with Gemini 3.5 Transcribe
  the captions stay in the language you spoke.
- When the caption server is busy, the progress card shows how long to wait
  and your place in line, and counts down. Your captions start by themselves;
  Cancel takes you out of the line.

NEW: AUTOCAPTIONS - YOUR SCRIPT
- A "Your script" field under Language. Paste the script you read (or load a
  text file) and the captions take its spelling and wording wherever you said
  those words, so names and brands come out the way you wrote them.
- What you really said usually wins: a clearly different word stays as you
  said it, ad-libs are kept, and common opposites (before/after, on/off) and
  different numbers are not swapped in. Words that look alike, and numbers you
  say as words, can still take the script's version, so check those captions.
- If the script does not match the recording, it is not used and the panel
  tells you so. It is not applied to translated captions.

NEW: AUTOCAPTIONS - EDITABLE TEMPLATE
- Style & Editor > STYLE > OUTPUT: choose Text layers (as before) or Editable
  template.
- Editable template: pick one of 10 Glow looks, an animation, fade out, 1-4
  words per caption, size, height and arrangement. Each caption becomes a
  template layer you can restyle later in Essential Properties.
- Needs After Effects 2026 (26.0) or newer, with the project's expression
  engine set to JavaScript (File > Project Settings > Expressions; the panel
  tells you if it is not).

CHANGED: AUTOCAPTIONS
- Nothing to caption picked? When no layer is selected and the Source media box
  is empty, the panel asks whether to caption the whole composition. It mixes
  every audible footage layer and captions from the start of the comp (muted,
  precomp, time-remapped, reversed and missing-footage layers are skipped;
  press Log on the progress card to see how many, and why). The question only
  comes while the Source media box is empty; once it shows a file (after
  Detect Selected, Browse or an earlier run), close and reopen the panel to
  empty it.
- Captioning the same audio again with the same settings reuses the transcript:
  no waiting, no upload, no credits used. Tick "Transcribe again" (under More
  options) for a fresh one.
- A simpler tab: the less used settings (Roman script, gaps, Transcribe again)
  sit under More options, and the engine note sits under the Transcription
  Model row, which is no longer squeezed.

CHANGED: FLEX STUDIO (BETA) - DOWNLOAD, THEN INSTALL
- FX & ADV RIGS > FLEX STUDIO (BETA): when the app is not on your computer, the
  button now reads DOWNLOAD FLEX STUDIO (BETA) with its size (it was INSTALL
  FLEX STUDIO (BETA)). It downloads the app, then asks "Install it now?"
  (Install / Not now).
- The app goes into Applications (Mac) or your Programs folder (Windows) and
  needs no password. Its After Effects effect, Flex Studio Warp, comes with it:
  the first time a Flex effect goes in, your computer asks once (your Mac
  password, or OK in the Windows administrator prompt), and After Effects loads
  it after a restart (RESTART AFTER EFFECTS, or LATER).
- The UPDATE chip downloads and asks the same way.

NEW: FLEX STUDIO (BETA) 2.0.0-beta.2 (the app)
- Workspaces across the top: PRO (everything), TRACK, ROTOSCOPE and COLOR
  GRADING.
- COLOR GRADING brings the Flex STUDIO grading tools into the app and grades the
  clip you have open. Save Grade keeps the grade with that clip; to use it in
  After Effects, export it as a .cube LUT and apply that LUT.
- The tool keys can follow After Effects: Edit > Preferences > KEY SHORTCUTS,
  set KEYS to AFTER EFFECTS.
- Clips open at once: the app is usable while the rest of the clip is still
  being read.
- OPEN now also takes a precomp, an adjustment layer (with the layers under
  it), a solid, a text or a shape layer, as it looks in the comp. The window
  opens straight away while After Effects sends the frames (32-bit projects
  render a movie first).

FIXED
- Theme effects (trails, button effects) were missing on many Windows PCs.
  - The first-run hardware check could switch Optimised Mode on by itself on
    ordinary Windows computers. Those are checked once more and get the effects
    back, unless they really are below what After Effects needs (less than
    16 GB of RAM, a 4-thread processor, or no graphics acceleration); those
    stay in Optimised Mode, which you can turn off in Flex Settings.
  - With Windows animation effects off, the anime themes now run a lighter
    version (a short swing on each click, no cursor trail) instead of nothing.
  - In Flex Settings, a new line under Ambient Effects says what is holding the
    effects back, with a TURN OFF OPTIMISED MODE button when that is the reason.
- The language search said "Search 133 languages"; it is 132.

NEW: OPTIONAL LOCATION FOR LICENCE PROTECTION
- The panel asks once whether you allow it to send this computer's location
  (or, when your system can't give one, roughly your city from your internet
  connection), to protect your licence: to spot one key used from far-apart
  places at the same time. It is optional: "No thanks" keeps every feature
  working.
- Change your mind any time: Flex Settings > PRIVACY. Turning it off also
  deletes what was stored for your licence and computer.
- Kept for 12 months, then deleted. Never sold, and never shared with anyone
  else: our hosting provider only stores it for us, and we would hand it over
  only if the law requires it. Details: captureflex.in/privacy

UNCHANGED
- Licensing, activation and every link work exactly as before.
- Every other tab and tool works exactly as in 9.2.9.
