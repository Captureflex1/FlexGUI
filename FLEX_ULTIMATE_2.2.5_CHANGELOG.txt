FLEX ULTIMATE FOR PREMIERE PRO - WHAT'S NEW
Version 2.2.5  -  October 2026

The extra speech engines in AUTO CAPTIONS now download from Flex's own mirror:
no Hugging Face account or token needed.

BEFORE YOU START
- Install over your current version with your usual ZXP installer, then restart
  Premiere Pro once.
- Your quick effects, presets, settings and downloaded templates are kept.

CHANGED: SPEECH ENGINES DOWNLOAD WITHOUT A HUGGING FACE ACCOUNT
- AI4Bharat IndicConformer, SraVaani and NVIDIA Canary download from the Flex
  mirror the first time you use them. You no longer need a Hugging Face account
  or access token for IndicConformer and SraVaani.
- Every download is checked against the checksums built into the panel before it
  is used. If the mirror cannot be reached, the engine falls back to Hugging Face
  as before.
- Sizes on first use: SraVaani 0.9 GB, IndicConformer 2.4 GB, Canary 3.7 GB.
  Each copy includes its model card, licence and attribution.

UNCHANGED
- Licensing, activation and every link work exactly as before.
- Every other tab and tool works exactly as in 2.2.4.
